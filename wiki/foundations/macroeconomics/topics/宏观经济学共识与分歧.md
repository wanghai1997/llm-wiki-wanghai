# 宏观经济学共识与分歧

Mankiw 9e's Epilogue, "What We Know and What We Don't," presents a provisional summary of where macroeconomics stands after the 2008 crisis. The picture is one of **partial consensus on methodology** alongside **deep disagreements on policy and interpretation**.

## Six Areas of Broad Consensus

1. **Long-run growth is driven by productivity**
   - Solow/Romer framework is broadly accepted
   - Institutional and policy factors matter for TFP

2. **Money is neutral in the long run**
   - Classical dichotomy holds for long-run price levels
   - Inflation is ultimately a monetary phenomenon (Friedman)

3. **Expectations matter**
   - Rational expectations is the default modeling assumption
   - Policy effectiveness depends on credibility and communication

4. **Short-run fluctuations involve demand and supply**
   - Both AD and AS shifts drive business cycles
   - Sticky prices/wages explain non-neutrality in the short run

5. **Financial markets can amplify shocks**
   - Post-2008 consensus: credit frictions matter
   - Financial accelerator, bank runs, and leverage cycles are real

6. **Policy faces tradeoffs**
   - Inflation vs. unemployment (short run)
   - Rules vs. discretion
   - Stability vs. moral hazard

## Four Major Disagreements

### 1. The Source of Business Cycles

| View | Representative | Core Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Real Business Cycle** | Prescott, Charles Plosser | Technology shocks drive everything; policy is neutral |
| **New Keynesian** | Mankiw, Blanchard | Demand shocks + nominal rigidities; policy matters |
| **Financial/Minskyian** | Bernanke, Minsky | Financial instability is endogenous; cycles are credit-driven |

### 2. The Role of Stabilization Policy

| View | Representative | Core Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Activist Keynesian** | Krugman, Summers | Fiscal policy is powerful, especially at ZLB |
| **Monetarist/Skeptical** | Taylor, Cochrane | Rules-based monetary policy; fiscal policy is often ineffective |
| **Austrian** | Hayek, Rothbard | Recessions are necessary corrections; intervention prolongs pain |

### 3. The Validity of DSGE Models

| View | Core Claim |
|---|---|
| **Pro-DSGE** | Microfoundations are essential; models are improving |
| **Skeptical** | DSGE is too simplified to be useful for policy; crises are inherently outside model scope |
| **Post-Keynesian/MMT** | Mainstream models are ideologically biased toward market efficiency |

### 4. Inequality, Climate, and Distribution

Mainstream macro (including Mankiw 9e) largely abstracts from:
- **Wealth inequality**: Piketty (2014) challenge to representative agent models
- **Climate change**: Green macroeconomics, stranded assets, carbon budgets
- **Gender/racial gaps**: Labor market dynamics differ across demographics
- **Global development**: Most macro theory is calibrated to OECD economies

## The Post-2016 Developments

Developments since Mankiw 9e (2016) publication:

1. **Secular stagnation**: Summers (2013) argues $r^*$ has fallen persistently, limiting monetary policy space
2. **HANK models**: Heterogeneous agent models revolutionize fiscal multiplier estimates
3. **MMT**: Modern Monetary Theory challenges debt sustainability orthodoxy
4. **CBDC and digital money**: Central bank digital currencies may transform monetary policy
5. **COVID-19**: Largest peacetime fiscal expansion; inflation surge challenges 2% target consensus
6. **AI and automation**: Potential for large labor market disruption; macro models not yet adapted

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **共识的"虚假中和"**: 教材声称宏观经济学已达成"广泛共识"，但后凯恩斯主义(Minsky金融不稳定、Kalecki利润驱动增长)、MMT(政府赤字不危险)、生态经济学(增长极限)完全缺席。这些不是边缘观点——它们代表了重要的学术和政策传统。
- **方法论的单一化**: DSGE模型成为"标准"，但实证文献显示其预测力有限。其他方法(向量自回归、叙事识别、机器学习预测)在特定问题上可能更优。
- **教材的时效性局限**: 2016年版无法预见COVID-19、2021-2023通胀 surge、AI革命。这些事件正在重塑宏观共识。

### 知识缺口

1. **HANK模型**: 2010s后发展，考虑不同收入/财富群体的差异化行为，对财政乘数估计产生革命性影响
2. **现代货币理论(MMT)**: Wray/Kelton框架——货币主权政府不受预算约束，通胀是唯一限制
3. **绿色宏观经济学**: 气候变化作为宏观经济冲击(碳预算、搁浅资产、绿色投资需求)
4. **数字货币与CBDC**: 比特币、稳定币、央行数字货币对货币需求、货币政策传导的影响
5. **长期停滞(Secular Stagnation)**: Summers (2013) 提出; $r^*$ 持续下降导致的货币政策空间枯竭
6. **不平等与宏观**: Piketty (2014) 财富不平等动态; 分配对总需求的非线性效应
7. **中国模式**: 中国宏观政策框架(多目标制、MLF/LPR/存款准备金率工具)与教材美式框架差异巨大

### [regional-bias] 中国官方立场:对教材共识的局部接受 + 局部修正

[[孙国峰]] 《健全现代货币政策框架》(2021) 作为 PBoC 货币政策司司长的官方表述,系统地展现了中国对教材"共识"的态度:

| 教材共识 | 中国官方立场(孙国峰) | 偏离方向 |
|---|---|---|
| 货币长期中性 | 接受(物价稳定是货币政策最终目标之一)| 一致 |
| 通胀是货币现象 | 接受(M2 与名义 GDP 基本匹配)+ 修正(用社融替代 M2 作为关键观察)| 部分修正 |
| 期望理性、规则化 | 部分接受(LPR 形成机制部分规则化)+ 部分相机抉择([[结构性货币政策工具]])| 显著偏离 |
| 政策工具中性 | **明确拒绝**(精准滴灌 / 定向支持 / 重点领域)| 根本分歧 |
| 央行独立性 | 部分接受(技术独立)+ 党的领导原则 | 显著偏离 |
| 财政与货币分离 | **强烈坚持**(反对[[财政赤字货币化]])| 一致(立场更坚定)|
| 金融不稳定理论 | 接受([[宏观审慎评估MPA]] / 双支柱框架)| 一致 |
| 内生货币论 | **接受**(BoE 2014 框架的中国采纳)| 修正(优于教材的外生货币论)|

**关键创新点**:孙国峰文提出 [[央行三大约束-流动性资本利率|"流动性 / 资本 / 利率"三大约束方法论]],代替单一货币乘数模型——是后凯恩斯学派 / 内生货币论的中国本土化变体。
