# 消费理论

The **microfoundations of consumption** trace how economists' understanding of household spending evolved from Keynes's simple rule of thumb to sophisticated intertemporal optimization models and, most recently, to behavioral approaches that incorporate psychological realism. Mankiw 9e Ch 16 presents this as a **historical progression** of five models, each addressing the limitations of its predecessor.

## The Five Models: A Hierarchy

```
Keynes (1936)          →  Current income drives consumption
    ↓
Fisher (1930)          →  Intertemporal optimization: consumption smoothing
    ↓
Modigliani (1954)      →  Life-cycle: saving for retirement
    ↓
Friedman (1957)        →  Permanent income: distinguishing temporary vs. permanent shocks
    ↓
Hall (1978)            →  Random walk: rational expectations + PIH
    ↓
Laibson (1997+)        →  Behavioral: present bias, self-control, mental accounting
```

## Model 1: Keynes's Consumption Function

$$C = \bar{C} + cY, \quad 0 < c < 1$$

- Consumption depends on **current disposable income**
- Marginal propensity to consume (MPC): $c \approx 0.9$ (short-run)
- **The consumption puzzle**: Kuznets (1946) found long-run MPC ≈ 1.0 — consumption tracks income one-for-one in the long run, contradicting Keynes's short-run estimate

See [[Keynes消费函数]]

## Model 2: Fisher's Intertemporal Choice

Fisher (1930) introduced the **two-period consumption model**:

$$\max_{c_1, c_2} U(c_1, c_2) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad c_1 + \frac{c_2}{1+r} = y_1 + \frac{y_2}{1+r}$$

**Key insight**: Consumers choose consumption to **smooth utility** over time, not to match income in each period.

**The consumption Euler equation**:
$$MRS = 1 + r$$

Implications:
- A consumer with rising income should save when young and dissave when old
- Interest rate changes have **income** and **substitution** effects
- Borrowing constraints can prevent optimal smoothing

See [[Fisher跨期选择模型]]

## Model 3: Modigliani's Life-Cycle Hypothesis (LCH)

$$C = \frac{W + R \cdot Y}{T}$$

- Consumers smooth consumption over their **entire lifetime**
- Working years: income > consumption → accumulate wealth
- Retirement years: consumption > income → decumulate wealth
- Aggregate saving depends on **demographics** and **income growth**

See [[生命周期假说]]

## Model 4: Friedman's Permanent Income Hypothesis (PIH)

$$C = kY^p$$

- Income has two components: $Y = Y^p + Y^t$
  - **Permanent income** ($Y^p$): expected long-run average income
  - **Transitory income** ($Y^t$): temporary deviations
- Consumers spend a fraction $k$ of permanent income; transitory income is mostly saved
- **MPC out of permanent income** ≈ 1; **MPC out of transitory income** ≈ 0

See [[永久收入假说]]

## Model 5: Hall's Random Walk Hypothesis

Under PIH + rational expectations:

$$C_{t+1} = C_t + \varepsilon_{t+1}$$

where $\varepsilon$ is an unpredictable shock.

**Implication**: Only **unanticipated** changes in lifetime resources affect consumption. Systematic, predictable policy has no effect on consumption.

See [[随机游走假说]]

## Model 6: Laibson's Behavioral Consumption

Introducing **present bias** ($\beta$-$\delta$ preferences):

- Consumers systematically overweight immediate gratification
- Explains: low retirement savings, credit card debt, high MPC out of rebates
- Policy implication: **commitment devices** (illiquid savings, automatic enrollment) may be more effective than price incentives

See [[行为消费理论]]

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 各模型的适用范围不清

教材将五种模型并置为"发展脉络",但未明确各自的适用条件:
- **凯恩斯**: 适用于短期、流动性约束强的家庭
- **Fisher/Modigliani/Friedman**: 适用于中产阶级、信贷市场发达的经济体
- **Laibson**: 适用于理解特定行为异常

不同国家、不同时期、不同收入群体的消费行为可能由不同模型主导。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 微观-宏观的桥接失败

微观消费理论(Fisher优化)预测平滑消费路径,但宏观数据显示:
- **消费过度敏感**: 消费对当前收入的响应大于PIH预测(Excess Sensitivity, Flavin 1981)
- **消费过度平滑**: 消费对永久收入冲击的响应小于PIH预测(Excess Smoothness, Campbell-Deaton 1989)
- **异质性**:  aggregate结果掩盖了巨大的个体差异

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 行为经济学宏观整合不足

Ch 16-5引入行为消费理论,但:
- 仅限消费;投资、货币需求、劳动供给等行为修正缺失
- 无DSGE整合;主流新凯恩斯模型仍假设完全理性
- 政策含义未展开:若消费者有时间不一致偏好,"助推"可能比利率政策更有效

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 住房财富的遗漏

教材的消费理论以金融财富和人力资本为主,但:
- 住房是美国家庭最大的财富组成部分
- 住房财富对消费的影响(MPC ≈ 0.03-0.09)可能与金融 wealth 不同
- 2000s房地产泡沫和崩溃对消费的冲击远超标准模型预测

### 数据空白

1. **HANK模型(异质性代理人)**: 2010s后Kaplan-Moll-Violante (2018)发现不同wealth群体的MPC差异巨大(0-0.8),aggregate结果与representative agent模型截然不同
2. **中国高储蓄之谜**: 中国储蓄率~45%,远超任何标准模型预测——预防性动机、教育竞争、住房购买、社会保障不足共同作用
3. **数字消费的测度**: 免费数字服务(Google, Facebook)的消费者剩余未进入GDP,消费函数框架未处理
4. **气候消费**: 极端天气(热浪、洪水)对消费模式的影响——新领域
