# 短期经济波动

**Short-run economic fluctuations**—commonly called **business cycles**—are the recurrent but irregular movements in aggregate economic activity around its long-run trend. This topic page integrates the core models and concepts from Mankiw 9e Part IV (Ch 10-13): the AD-AS framework, the IS-LM model, and the Mundell-Fleming model for open economies.

## The Central Question

Why does output deviate from its long-run potential? The classical model (Part II-III) assumes flexible prices and market clearing, implying output always equals potential. The Keynesian short-run framework relaxes this assumption: **prices are sticky**, and **aggregate demand determines output**.

## The Model Hierarchy

### Level 1: AD-AS (Ch 10)

The [[AD-AS模型]] is the organizing framework:
- **Aggregate Demand (AD)**: Downward-sloping relationship between price level and output demanded
- **Short-Run Aggregate Supply (SRAS)**: Upward-sloping due to price stickiness
- **Long-Run Aggregate Supply (LRAS)**: Vertical at potential output

**Key insights**:
- Demand shocks move output and prices in the same direction
- Supply shocks create stagflation (recession + inflation)
- The economy self-corrects to LRAS, but slowly

### Level 2: IS-LM (Ch 11-12)

The [[IS-LM模型]] provides the microfoundations for AD:
- **IS curve**: Goods market equilibrium ($Y = C + I + G$)
- **LM curve**: Money market equilibrium ($M/P = L(r, Y)$)
- **Policy analysis**: Fiscal policy shifts IS; monetary policy shifts LM

**Key insights**:
- Fiscal expansion crowds out investment via higher interest rates
- Monetary expansion stimulates output via lower interest rates
- At the [[流动性陷阱]], monetary policy fails and fiscal policy is fully effective

### Level 3: Mundell-Fleming (Ch 13)

The [[Mundell-Fleming模型]] extends IS-LM to the open economy:
- Perfect capital mobility fixes $r = r^*$
- Exchange rate becomes the equilibrating variable
- Policy effectiveness depends critically on the exchange rate regime

**Key insights**:
- Floating rates: Monetary policy effective, fiscal policy ineffective
- Fixed rates: Fiscal policy effective, monetary policy ineffective
- The [[不可能三角]]: Cannot have free capital, fixed rates, and monetary independence simultaneously

## From Closed to Open Economy

| Dimension | IS-LM (Closed) | Mundell-Fleming (Open) |
|---|---|---|
| Interest rate | Endogenous | Fixed at $r^*$ |
| Exchange rate | Not applicable | Endogenous |
| Monetary policy | Shifts LM | Shifts LM*; affects $e$ |
| Fiscal policy | Shifts IS; crowds out via $r$ | Shifts IS*; crowds out via $e$ (floating) or accommodated (fixed) |

## Key Historical Episodes

### The Great Depression (1929–1933)
- Catastrophic negative [[需求冲击]]: wealth destruction, banking panic, gold standard constraints
- Classical self-correction failed; wages and prices did not fall enough to restore full employment
- Keynes's *General Theory* (1936) was the intellectual response

### The 1970s Stagflation
- OPEC oil shocks: adverse [[供给冲击]]
- Policymakers faced the painful inflation-unemployment tradeoff
- Keynesian demand management proved inadequate against supply shocks

### The 1990s Boom
- IT productivity surge: favorable [[供给冲击]]
- Clinton fiscal consolidation + Greenspan monetary easing: successful policy mix
- "Non-inflationary growth"—the Goldilocks economy

### The 2008 Financial Crisis
- Housing bust + financial panic: demand and financial supply shock
- Liquidity trap reached in US, EU, Japan
- Unprecedented fiscal stimulus + quantitative easing

### COVID-19 (2020–2022)
- Simultaneous supply (lockdowns) and demand (precautionary saving) shocks
- Massive coordinated fiscal-monetary response
- Inflation surge as supply chains failed during demand recovery

### Level 4: Phillips Curve and Inflation-Unemployment Tradeoff (Ch 14)

Ch 14 completes the short-run framework by providing the **microfoundations of aggregate supply** and deriving the [[Phillips曲线]] as the empirical expression of the inflation-unemployment relationship.

**Microfoundations of SRAS** (Ch 14-1): Three theories explain why the SRAS slopes upward:
- [[粘性工资理论]]: Nominal wage contracts create real wage fluctuations
- [[粘性价格理论]]: Menu costs prevent immediate price adjustment
- [[不完全信息理论]]: Lucas (1972) signal extraction—producers confuse aggregate and relative price changes

All three converge on: $Y = \bar{Y} + \alpha(P - P^e)$

**The Phillips Curve** (Ch 14-2): The intellectual history from the original Phillips (1958) empirical relationship through:
- **Friedman-Phelps natural rate critique** (1968): Long-run Phillips curve is vertical at $u^n$
- **Lucas-Sargent rational expectations revolution** (1970s): Even short-run tradeoff vanishes for anticipated policy
- **New Keynesian resurrection** (1990s): Forward-looking NKPC with Calvo pricing

**Key policy concepts**:
- [[自然率假说]]: Unemployment returns to $u^n$ in the long run regardless of inflation
- [[适应性预期]]: Inflation expectations based on past realizations
- [[理性预期]]: Agents use all available information optimally
- [[Lucas批判]]: Policy evaluation cannot rely on historical estimated parameters
- [[无代价反通胀]]: Credible regime changes can reduce inflation without recession
- [[滞后效应]]: Short-run unemployment may permanently raise $u^n$
- [[牺牲率]]: The output cost of reducing inflation
- [[反通胀的产出代价]]: Volcker vs. Greenspan episodes

## The Tension: Short Run vs. Long Run

Mankiw 9e adopts the **neoclassical synthesis**:
- **Short run**: Keynesian (sticky prices, demand-determined output)
- **Long run**: Classical (flexible prices, supply-determined output)

But this raises unresolved questions:
- How does the economy transition from short run to long run?
- What determines the speed of price adjustment?
- Can short-run shocks have permanent (hysteresis) effects on long-run potential?

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 古典二分法 vs Keynesian 非二分法:教材的"双重标准"

Mankiw 9e 在同一本教材中使用两套互不兼容的均衡概念:
- Part II-III (Ch 3-9): 长期价格完全灵活,产出由供给面决定
- Part IV (Ch 10-13): 短期价格完全粘性,产出由需求面决定

**问题**:教材从未解释价格从粘性到灵活的**过渡机制**。这是新凯恩斯主义文献的核心问题(Calvo定价、菜单成本、协调失灵),但教材将其简化为"短期看Keynes,长期看古典"的时间分割,回避了微基础。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] IS-LM作为"教学模型" vs "政策工具"的张力

IS-LM是本科教学的核心模型,但现代央行决策几乎不直接使用它:
- **美联储**:FRB/US模型、DSGE模型、大量实时数据
- **欧央行**:NAWM、双支柱框架
- **中国人民银行**:多目标、多工具的综合框架

教材将IS-LM呈现为"宏观经济学的主模型",但实际是"教学入门模型",这一差距未向学生明确说明。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] Mundell-Fleming的"小型开放经济"假设

Mundell-Fleming假设小型开放经济(利率=世界利率),但:
- **美国**:联邦基金利率由美联储独立设定
- **中国**:资本账户管制使国内利率大幅偏离世界利率
- **欧元区**:作为整体是大型经济体

教材将小型开放经济模型呈现为"开放经济的标准分析",但对大型经济体不适用。

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 流动性陷阱的教材处理过时

教材称"in the liquidity trap, monetary policy is ineffective",但2008后:
- **QE**:央行购买长期资产压低期限溢价,不经过短期利率
- **前瞻指引**:政策沟通改变预期
- **负利率**:欧元区、日本实施了负政策利率

这些现代工具超越了传统IS-LM的二元框架。

### 数据空白

1. **金融加速器**:Bernanke-Gertler-Gilchrist (1999) 发现信贷摩擦放大波动,但教材完全未出现
2. **滞后效应(hysteresis)**:短期衰退可能永久降低潜在产出(Blanchard-Summers 1986),但教材假设长期自动回归LRAS
3. **全球金融周期**:Rey (2015) 发现资本流动使各国货币政策 increasingly 同步,挑战 Mundell-Fleming 的国别分析
4. **数字经济与波动**:数字服务的即时性和可扩展性可能改变经济周期性质
5. **气候变化与波动**:极端天气事件 increasingly 作为供给冲击出现
