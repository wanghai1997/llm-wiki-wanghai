# Edmund Phelps

> **一句话画像**:Edmund Phelps(1933-2023),美国宏观经济学家,哥伦比亚大学教授,**2006 年诺贝尔经济学奖**得主。1968 年与 [[Milton Friedman]] 同时独立提出**自然失业率假说**(Natural Rate Hypothesis),预言长期 Phillips 曲线垂直,货币政策无法持续降低失业率——该预言在 1970s 滞胀中得到验证,重塑了宏观经济学对通胀-失业权衡的理解。

## 核心学术贡献

### 1. 自然率假说(1967–68)

- **Phillips 曲线修正**:1958 年 A.W. Phillips 发现英国工资增长与失业负相关;Samuelson-Solow 1960 将其改写为通胀-失业权衡
- **Phelps 的质疑**:引入**预期调整**——工人与企业根据预期通胀设定工资/价格;只有当实际通胀 > 预期通胀时,失业率才暂时低于自然率
- **1967 论文**: *"Phillips Curves, Expectations of Inflation and Optimal Unemployment Over Time"*——首个正式的搜索理论模型,从微观层面推导自然率
- **长期垂直 Phillips 曲线**:货币政策只能制造意外通胀来暂时压低失业,长期回归自然率
- **与 Friedman 的关系**:Friedman 1968 AEA 主席演讲同时提出几乎相同的论点;两人共享"自然率"概念的首创权,但 Phelps 的模型更形式化
- 详见 [[自然率假说]] / [[自然失业率]] / [[Phillips曲线]]

### 2. 经济增长中的黄金律 (1961)

Phelps's 1961 paper *"The Golden Rule of Accumulation: A Fable for Growthmen"* (American Economic Review) derived the condition for the **consumption-maximizing steady state** in the Solow growth model.

**The Problem**: Given a production function $y = f(k)$ and capital accumulation $\dot{k} = sf(k) - (\delta + n)k$, what saving rate $s$ maximizes steady-state consumption per capita?

**Steady-state consumption**:
$$c^* = f(k^*) - (\delta + n)k^*$$

**First-order condition** for maximum:
$$\frac{dc^*}{dk^*} = f'(k^*) - (\delta + n) = 0$$

**The Golden Rule**:
$$MPK = \delta + n$$

With technological progress at rate $g$:
$$MPK = \delta + n + g \quad \text{or equivalently} \quad MPK - \delta = n + g$$

**Interpretation**: The net marginal product of capital must equal the economy's growth rate. At this point, no generation is asked to sacrifice for the benefit of others—each consumes what it would want previous generations to have consumed.

**Dynamic efficiency criterion**:
- If $MPK - \delta > n + g$: economy is **below** Golden Rule (capital scarce); higher saving raises long-run consumption
- If $MPK - \delta < n + g$: economy is **above** Golden Rule (**dynamically inefficient**); lower saving raises consumption at all points in time

Phelps named the condition by analogy to the biblical Golden Rule. It is also the steady state that a **social planner with zero discount rate** would choose. In the Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans optimal growth framework (not covered in Mankiw), the optimal capital stock is typically **below** the Golden Rule because households have positive time preference ($\rho > 0$).

### 3. 结构主义宏观经济学

- 1994 *Structural Slumps*:试图从微观结构(劳动市场制度、激励、信息)解释失业的持续性
- 与标准新凯恩斯模型的需求侧解释形成对比

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **"自然率"的误导性名称**:Phelps-Friedman 借用 Wicksell 的"自然利率"隐喻,但失业率远非"自然"——它高度内生于劳动力市场制度(失业保险、最低工资、工会密度、培训政策)。后凯恩斯派批评"自然率"是为放任政策辩护的意识形态构造。[CONTRADICTION]
- **自然率的不稳定性**:1970-2020 美国自然率估计在 4.5%-6.5% 间漂移,使"垂直 Phillips 曲线"的预测精度存疑;2008 后美国失业率长期高于估计自然率,引发"滞后效应"(hysteresis)讨论。
- **滞后效应的挑战**:Blanchard-Summers 1986 提出欧洲高失业可能具有滞后性——短期衰退 → 自然率永久性上升——这直接 contradict Phelps-Friedman 自然率稳定的假设。
- **regional-bias**:Phelps 的理论框架以美国/欧洲劳动市场为基准,对发展中国家非正规就业、中国户籍制度、印度劳动法等制度差异缺乏解释力。
