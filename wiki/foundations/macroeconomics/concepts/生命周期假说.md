# 生命周期假说

The **life-cycle hypothesis (LCH)**, developed by [[Franco Modigliani]] and Richard Brumberg (1954), posits that individuals plan their consumption and savings behavior over their entire lifetime. Rather than consuming based on current income (as Keynes assumed), households seek to maintain a stable consumption path by saving during working years and dis-saving during retirement.

## The Core Mechanism

### Lifetime Planning

A consumer with lifetime $T$ years, $R$ working years, annual labor income $Y$, and initial wealth $W$:

$$C = \frac{W + R \cdot Y}{T}$$

**Key features**:
- **Consumption is proportional to lifetime resources**: $C \propto (W + RY)$
- **The MPC out of wealth**: $\partial C / \partial W = 1/T$ (small, because wealth is spread over many years)
- **The MPC out of income**: $\partial C / \partial Y = R/T$ (less than 1, because only working-year income enters lifetime resources)

### The Life-Cycle Pattern

```
Income/Consumption
    │
Y   │     ╱╲  Labor income
    │    ╱  ╲
C   │───╱────╲───  Smooth consumption
    │  ╱      ╲
    │ ╱        ╲___ Retirement income
    └────────────────→ Age
      Working  Retired
```

**Working years** ($0$ to $R$):
- Income > Consumption → **Accumulate wealth**
- Wealth builds as savings plus returns

**Retirement years** ($R$ to $T$):
- Consumption > Income → **Decumulate wealth**
- Wealth drawn down to finance consumption

### The Saving Rate

The individual saving rate during working years:

$$s = \frac{Y - C}{Y} = 1 - \frac{R}{T} - \frac{W}{T \cdot Y}$$

- Younger workers save more (longer retirement to fund)
- Higher income growth increases saving (current income > lifetime average)
- Unexpected wealth (inheritance, stock market gains) is spread over remaining lifetime

## Aggregate Implications

### The Role of Demographics

Aggregate saving depends on the **age structure** of the population:
- **Young population**: Many savers → high aggregate saving
- **Aging population**: Many dis-savers → low or negative aggregate saving
- **Japan's high saving** (1980s): Rapid income growth + young population
- **Japan's declining saving** (2000s+): Aging population + stagnant incomes

### The Role of Income Growth

If income grows over time:
- Young workers earn more than the lifetime average of older cohorts
- Young savers outweigh old dis-savers
- Result: **Positive aggregate saving even with stable demographics**

**Formula**: Aggregate saving rate ≈ $g \times \text{average wealth-to-income ratio}$, where $g$ is the growth rate of per-capita income.

## Empirical Evidence

### Supporting Evidence

- **Cross-section data**: Higher-income households have higher saving rates (because they are younger and further from retirement)
- **Retirement consumption**: Consumption does not collapse at retirement (contradicting naive Keynesian prediction)
- **International comparison**: Countries with rapid income growth have higher saving rates

### Challenges

- **Buffer-stock saving (Carroll 1992, 1997)**: Precautionary motives (uncertainty about income, health, longevity) may dominate life-cycle motives for many households, especially the poor
- **Bequest motive**: Kotlikoff-Summers (1981) argued that much US wealth is inherited, not life-cycle accumulated. If consumers care about heirs, they may not fully decumulate
- **Housing wealth**: Home equity is illiquid and provides housing services, complicating the pure wealth-income distinction
- **Mandatory retirement savings**: 401(k) and Social Security blur the line between voluntary life-cycle saving and forced saving

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 退休消费下降之谜

LCH预测退休消费应与工作期间持平(平滑),但实证显示:
- **美国**: 退休消费下降~10-30%(Aguiar-Hurst 2005)
- **解释**: 工作相关支出(通勤、服装)减少;健康限制活动;偏好变化(闲暇替代消费)
- **Feenstein-Schaller (2022)**: 晚年健康支出不确定性可能解释消费下降

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 缓冲库存储蓄vs生命周期

Carroll (1997) buffer-stock模型发现:
- 对低收入/高不确定性家庭,预防性储蓄 > 生命周期储蓄
- 目标财富-收入比由不确定性决定,而非退休年龄
- 这些家庭的行为更接近"意外之财储蓄"而非"退休规划"

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 遗赠动机的强度

Kotlikoff-Summers (1981) 发现美国财富的2/3是遗赠(继承),非生命周期积累:
- 若真实,LCH仅解释1/3的财富积累
- 但遗赠可能是"意外"(未预期长寿)而非"有意"
- 最新估计(De Nardi-Yang 2014)显示有意遗赠仍占30-50%

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 住房财富的特殊性

LCH将财富视为同质,但:
- 住房财富( illiquid, 服务流)与金融 wealth (liquid, 可交易)对消费影响不同
- Campbell-Cocco (2007): 住房 wealth MPC ≈ 0.03-0.09; 金融 wealth MPC ≈ 0.02-0.04
- 2000s房地产泡沫: 住房 wealth 膨胀→消费 surge; 崩溃→消费 crash

### 数据空白

1. **中国的高储蓄率**: 储蓄率~45%,远超LCH预测——预防性动机(医疗、教育、养老)+住房购买+文化因素
2. **数字金融的影响**: 移动支付、P2P借贷是否改变生命周期储蓄行为?
3. **气候与消费**: 极端天气如何影响退休规划和跨期消费?
4. **AI与就业**: 自动化对终身收入路径的影响→生命周期储蓄如何调整?
