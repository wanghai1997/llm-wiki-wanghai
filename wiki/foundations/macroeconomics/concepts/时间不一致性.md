# 时间不一致性

**Time inconsistency**, formalized by [[Finn Kydland]] and [[Edward Prescott]] (1977), is the problem that a policymaker's optimal plan today may not remain optimal once tomorrow arrives. Rational agents anticipate this incentive to renege, undermining the credibility of policy announcements.

## The Core Problem

### A Simple Example: Monetary Policy

1. **Today**: The central bank announces it will maintain zero inflation to maximize welfare.
2. **Private sector**: Forms expectations of zero inflation, sets wages and prices accordingly.
3. **Tomorrow**: With expectations anchored at zero, the central bank faces a short-run Phillips curve tradeoff. A small surprise inflation ($\pi > 0$) would boost output above potential.
4. **The temptation**: The "optimal" policy today is to renege and create surprise inflation.
5. **Rational anticipation**: Knowing this, the private sector never believed zero inflation in the first place.
6. **Equilibrium**: Inflation is higher than optimal ($\bar{\pi} > 0$) with no output gain.

### Formal Representation

The policymaker maximizes:

$$W = -(\pi^2 + \lambda(y - y^*)^2)$$

subject to the Phillips curve $y = \bar{y} + \alpha(\pi - \pi^e)$.

Under **commitment** (binding promise): $\pi^* = 0$, $\pi^e = 0$, optimal welfare.

Under **discretion** (no commitment): $\pi = \frac{\alpha\lambda}{1+\alpha^2\lambda}\bar{y} > 0$, inflation bias.

## Commitment Devices

To solve time inconsistency, policymakers use:

1. **Central bank independence**: Insulate from short-term political pressure
2. **Inflation targeting**: Explicit numerical target with accountability
3. **Fixed rules**: Taylor rule or money growth rule
4. **Reputation**: Repeated interaction sustains low inflation if the cost of losing credibility is high
5. **Institutional design**: Conservative central bankers (Rogoff 1985) who care more about inflation than output

## Applications Beyond Monetary Policy

### Fiscal Policy
- Governments announce future tax increases to reduce deficits
- But when the time comes, raising taxes is politically costly
- Result: persistent deficits and debt accumulation

### Trade Policy
- Tariff reductions announced to gain credibility
- But protecting domestic industries remains tempting
- Result: trade agreements with dispute settlement mechanisms

### Regulation
- Promising not to bail out failing firms
- But when failure threatens systemic risk, bailout becomes optimal
- Result: moral hazard and "too big to fail"

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **规则的灵活性代价**: 完全承诺在极端冲击(2008, 2020)下可能是灾难性的。需要"有退出条款的规则"——但这本身就是某种相机抉择。
- **声誉模型的脆弱性**: 声誉可以维持低通胀，但一旦丧失很难恢复。1990s新西兰央行从通胀目标转向更灵活框架，显示声誉是有限资产。
- **异质性代理人**: 代表性代理人假设下，所有人同等程度受通胀偏差影响。现实中，穷人(持有现金、固定名义工资)受意外通胀伤害更大——时间不一致性有分配维度。
- **开放经济维度**: 小国开放经济中，汇率承诺(货币局、美元化)是强承诺机制。但阿根廷2001年货币局崩溃证明，即使宪法级承诺在极端条件下也会破裂。
- **最优合约理论**: Walsh (1995) 的最优央行合约(通胀超标则扣减薪酬)在理论上可消除通胀偏差，但实践中几乎没有央行采用——政治可行性问题。
