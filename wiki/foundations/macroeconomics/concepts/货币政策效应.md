# 货币政策效应

**Monetary policy** refers to the central bank's management of money supply and interest rates to influence aggregate demand, output, and inflation. In the [[IS-LM模型]], monetary policy shifts the LM curve, affecting output and interest rates.

## Expansionary Monetary Policy

### Increase in Money Supply ($\Delta M > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Higher $M$ increases real money balances ($M/P$)
2. LM curve shifts right/down
3. New equilibrium: lower $r$, higher $Y$

**Effects**:
- Interest rate falls: $\Delta r < 0$
- Output increases: $\Delta Y > 0$
- **Crowding in**: Lower $r$ stimulates private investment
- Exchange rate depreciates (in open economy)

### The Monetary Transmission Mechanism

The traditional transmission channel:

$$M \uparrow \Rightarrow \frac{M}{P} \uparrow \Rightarrow r \downarrow \Rightarrow I \uparrow \Rightarrow Y \uparrow$$

This is the **interest rate channel** emphasized by Mankiw.

Modern central banking has expanded this to include:
- **Credit channel**: Bank lending capacity and firm balance sheets
- **Asset price channel**: Stock and housing wealth effects
- **Exchange rate channel**: Trade balance via currency depreciation
- **Expectations channel**: Forward guidance shaping future expectations

## Contractionary Monetary Policy

### Decrease in Money Supply ($\Delta M < 0$)
- LM shifts left/up
- Interest rate rises, output falls
- Used to combat inflation
- **Example**: Volcker disinflation 1979–1982 (see [[牺牲率]])

## Monetary Policy in Different Economic Conditions

### Normal Times
- Monetary expansion lowers rates and stimulates output
- Effectiveness depends on:
  - Slope of IS (investment sensitivity to $r$)
  - Interest sensitivity of money demand
  - Response of expectations

### Liquidity Trap ($r \approx 0$)
- LM is horizontal at the zero lower bound
- Conventional monetary policy becomes **ineffective**
- Cannot lower $r$ further
- **Unconventional tools**: Quantitative easing, forward guidance, negative policy rates

### Classical Case (Vertical LM)
- Monetary policy is **highly effective**
- Directly controls aggregate demand via money supply
- No crowding out of fiscal policy (because fiscal policy is already ineffective)

## Monetary vs. Fiscal Policy: A Comparison

| Dimension | Monetary Policy | Fiscal Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision lag** | Short (central bank meetings) | Long (legislative process) |
| **Implementation lag** | Immediate (open market operations) | Variable (spending plans, tax changes) |
| **Political independence** | Usually independent | Politically driven |
| **Reversibility** | Easy | Difficult (entitlements, political commitments) |
| **Distribution effects** | Indirect (asset prices, inflation) | Direct (who pays taxes, who receives spending) |
| **Zero lower bound** | May become ineffective | Remains effective |

## The US Experience: 1990s

Mankiw highlights the 1990s as an example of monetary-fiscal coordination:
- **Clinton fiscal consolidation**: IS shifted left
- **Fed monetary easing**: LM shifted right
- **Result**: Strong growth with falling interest rates
- This combination is sometimes called a "non-inflationary boom"

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 货币政策传导的现代复杂性

教材将货币政策传导简化为 $M \uparrow \Rightarrow r \downarrow \Rightarrow I \uparrow$,但现实中:

- **零利率下限**:2008后传统利率渠道失效,美联储转向QE
- **QE和资产组合再平衡**:央行购买长期资产压低期限溢价,不经过短期利率
- **信贷渠道**:银行资产负债表状况成为传导的关键摩擦(Bernanke-Gertler 1995)
- **信号渠道**:政策行动改变市场预期,独立于当前利率(Nakamura-Steinsson 2013)
- **财富不平等**:宽松货币政策推高资产价格,主要受益富人,可能加剧不平等

这些现代渠道无法被传统IS-LM框架充分捕捉。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 央行独立性的现代挑战

教材假设央行可独立执行货币政策,但:
- **政治压力**:特朗普2019年公开施压美联储降息
- **财政主导**:当政府债务/GDP极高时,央行被迫维持低利率以维持债务可持续性(Reinhart-Rogoff 2010)
- **气候和社会目标**:ECG和BoE纳入气候风险考量,挑战"价格稳定单一目标"
- **MMT挑战**:现代货币理论认为央行独立性是幻觉,财政赤字才是货币创造的主要来源

### [CONTRADICTION-3] "美联储中心主义"

Ch 12货币政策讨论几乎全部以美联储的联邦基金利率操作为中心:
- 欧央行的双支柱框架(货币+通胀)被忽略
- 中国人民银行的多种政策工具组合(MLF、LPR、存款准备金率)被忽略
- 日本银行的QQE(量化和质化宽松)先驱角色被忽略

教材将美联储的"单一利率目标"呈现为"货币政策"的通用形式,强化了美式案例霸权。

### 数据空白

1. **货币政策与金融稳定**:低利率可能催生资产泡沫,但教材将价格稳定与金融稳定分离
2. **数字货币**:CBDC可能根本改变货币政策传导机制,但教材2016年版无法涉及
3. **平均通胀目标制(AIT)**:Powell 2020年Jackson Hole演讲引入的新框架,允许通胀暂时超调以补偿过去不足
4. **新兴市场的货币政策**:资本流动、汇率压力和"原罪"(Eichengreen-Hausmann)使新兴市场货币政策远为复杂
