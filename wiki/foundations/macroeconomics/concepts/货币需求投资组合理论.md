# 货币需求投资组合理论

**The portfolio theory of money demand**, developed by [[James Tobin]] (1958), extends money demand analysis beyond the transactions motive to treat money as one asset within a diversified portfolio. It provides the microfoundations for the asset demand view of money.

## The Mean-Variance Framework

Tobin applied Markowitz's portfolio theory to money demand:

- **Money**: Risk-free return = 0 (or $-π$ in real terms), zero variance
- **Bonds**: Risky return = $i$, positive variance $\sigma^2$
- **Stocks**: Riskier return, higher variance

An investor chooses portfolio weights to maximize expected utility:

$$\max \; E[R_p] - \frac{\gamma}{2} Var(R_p)$$

## Money as a Risk-Free Asset

Money serves as the **risk-free anchor** in the portfolio:
- When expected returns on risky assets fall → demand for money rises
- When risk aversion increases (higher $\gamma$) → demand for money rises
- When inflation expectations rise → real return on money falls → demand falls

The key insight: money demand depends not just on $Y$ and $i$, but on the **entire distribution of asset returns** and investor risk preferences.

## Expected Inflation and the Opportunity Cost

The nominal return on money is typically zero (or slightly positive in some checking accounts). The real return is:

$$r_m = -\pi$$

where $\pi$ is inflation. Higher expected inflation:
- Increases the opportunity cost of holding money
- Shifts portfolio demand toward real assets and foreign currency
- Explains hyperinflation episodes where money demand collapses

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **货币不是真正的无风险资产**: 在高通胀环境中，货币的实际价值波动极大——其"无风险"仅限于名义值。Tobin框架不处理通胀风险本身。
- **行为投资组合理论**: 现实中投资者不使用均值-方差优化。Shefrin-Stauman (2000) 的行为投资组合理论显示投资者将资产分入"心理账户"，货币作为"安全层"——与Tobin的连续优化不同。
- **数字货币的挑战**: 比特币等加密货币的波动率($\sigma > 50\%$)使其无法作为Tobin框架中的"货币"——但稳定币(USDC、USDT)试图复制传统货币的无风险特征，同时提供链上可编程性。
- **regional-bias**: 投资组合选择以美国金融市场为默认(高流动性、多样化工具)。发展中国家(银行主导、股市不发达、外汇管制)中，"投资组合"的选择集极为有限，货币需求主要由交易和预防动机驱动。
