# 适应性预期

**Adaptive expectations** is a hypothesis about how economic agents form expectations of future variables (especially inflation) based on past realizations. Agents systematically adjust their forecasts in response to recent forecast errors:

$$\pi^e_t = \pi^e_{t-1} + \lambda(\pi_{t-1} - \pi^e_{t-1})$$

where $\lambda \in (0,1)$ is the speed of adjustment. In the simplest case ($\lambda = 1$): $\pi^e_t = \pi_{t-1}$.

## Mechanism and Implications

### The Inflation-Output Tradeoff

When combined with the expectations-augmented [[Phillips曲线]]:

$$\pi_t = \pi^e_t - \beta(u_t - u^n)$$

and adaptive expectations:

$$\pi^e_t = \pi_{t-1}$$

we obtain:

$$\pi_t - \pi_{t-1} = -\beta(u_t - u^n)$$

**The accelerationist result**: The change in inflation depends on the deviation of unemployment from the natural rate. To keep $u < u^n$, inflation must **continuously accelerate**.

### Dynamics of a Policy Experiment

Consider a central bank that permanently reduces unemployment below $u^n$:

| Period | $\pi^e$ | $\pi$ | $u$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2% | 2% | $u^n$ |
| 1 | 2% | 4% | $u^n - 1$ |
| 2 | 4% | 6% | $u^n - 1$ |
| 3 | 6% | 8% | $u^n - 1$ |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

Each period, workers catch up to last period's inflation, requiring even higher inflation this period to maintain the same unemployment gap.

## The Phillips Curve with Adaptive Expectations

The short-run Phillips curve **shifts upward** each period as expectations adjust:

- **Period 0**: $\pi = 2\% - \beta(u - u^n)$
- **Period 1**: $\pi = 4\% - \beta(u - u^n)$
- **Period 2**: $\pi = 6\% - \beta(u - u^n)$

**Long-run Phillips curve**: Vertical at $u = u^n$, regardless of the inflation rate.

## Historical Role in Macroeconomics

### Samuelson-Solow (1960)
The original Phillips curve implicitly assumed static expectations ($\pi^e = \text{constant}$), which Friedman and Phelps showed was untenable.

### Friedman-Phelps (1968)
Introduced the expectations-augmented Phillips curve with adaptive expectations as the baseline mechanism. This became the standard textbook treatment for decades.

### The 1970s Inflation
Adaptive expectations provided a natural explanation for why inflation kept rising during the 1970s: each round of expansionary policy raised inflation, which was then built into expectations, requiring even more expansion to achieve the same unemployment target.

## Comparison with Rational Expectations

| Feature | Adaptive Expectations | Rational Expectations |
|---|---|---|
| **Information used** | Only past realizations | All available information |
| **Forecast errors** | Systematic, predictable | Unsystematic, random |
| **Speed of adjustment** | Gradual ($\lambda$) | Instantaneous |
| **Policy effectiveness** | Short-run tradeoff exists | Only unanticipated policy matters |
| **Empirical realism** | Better fits gradual inflation changes | Better fits financial markets |
| **Theoretical coherence** | Ad hoc, no microfoundation | Consistent with optimization |

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 适应性预期低估了预期的前瞻性

适应性预期假设代理人只向后看,但现实中:
- **央行沟通**:美联储FOMC声明、点阵图(dot plot)直接影响公众预期
- **政策宣布效应**:宣布未来紧缩政策可能立即压低通胀预期,无需等待实际通胀下降
- **专业预测者**:Blue Chip、Survey of Professional Forecasters 显示预期具有显著前瞻性

仅使用适应性预期的模型在预测通胀转折点时系统性地滞后。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 学习速度 $\lambda$ 不稳定

教材假设固定的 $\lambda$,但实证估计显示:
- **高通胀时期**(1970s):$\lambda$ 较高(预期快速调整)
- **低通胀时期**(1990s-2000s):$\lambda$ 较低(预期惯性大)
- **锚定预期时期**(2010s后):$\lambda$ 极低(预期几乎不随实际通胀变化)

这意味着"适应性预期"本身不是稳定的结构参数,而是制度依赖的。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 与微观证据的矛盾

适应性预期在宏观模型中被广泛使用,但微观层面的学习行为:
- **实验室实验**:受试者往往比适应性预期更快速地调整,但不如完全理性
- **企业调查**:企业通胀预期受自身成本结构影响,而非仅过去CPI
- **消费者调查**:Michigan Survey of Consumers 显示通胀预期受食品/能源价格影响过大(过度反应局部信号)

### 数据空白

1. **混合预期模型**:现实中可能是适应性+理性的混合——但混合权重如何估计?
2. **异质性预期**:不同群体(工人、企业、投资者)的学习速度差异巨大
3. **锚定效应**:通胀目标制使预期"锚定"在目标值附近——如何建模这种制度转变?
4. **中国的预期形成**:中国央行缺乏通胀目标制框架,公众预期形成机制不明
