# 浮动汇率下的政策效应

Under a **floating exchange rate regime**, the exchange rate is determined by market forces without central bank intervention. In the [[Mundell-Fleming模型]], this regime produces strikingly different policy effectiveness results compared to a fixed exchange rate system.

## Monetary Policy Under Floating Rates

### Expansionary Monetary Policy ($\Delta M > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Money supply increases → LM* shifts right
2. Downward pressure on domestic interest rate
3. Capital outflows (seeking higher foreign returns)
4. Domestic currency **depreciates** ($e \uparrow$)
5. Depreciation improves net exports ($NX \uparrow$)
6. Output increases ($Y \uparrow$)

**Result**: Monetary policy is **highly effective** under floating rates.

```
Exchange Rate (e)
    │
    │  LM₁*  LM₂*
    │    │    │
    │    │    │   IS*
    │    │    │  /
    │    │    │ /
    └────┼────┼/─────→ Output (Y)
         Y₁  Y₂
         e₂  e₁ (depreciation: e↑)
```

### Transmission Channel

$$M \uparrow \Rightarrow \frac{M}{P} \uparrow \Rightarrow r \downarrow \Rightarrow \text{capital outflows} \Rightarrow e \uparrow \text{ (depreciation)} \Rightarrow NX \uparrow \Rightarrow Y \uparrow$$

The exchange rate channel amplifies the effect of monetary policy. This is why central banks in floating-rate economies (US, UK, Japan) rely primarily on monetary policy for stabilization.

## Fiscal Policy Under Floating Rates

### Expansionary Fiscal Policy ($\Delta G > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Government spending increases → IS* shifts right
2. Upward pressure on domestic interest rate
3. Capital inflows (attracted by higher returns)
4. Domestic currency **appreciates** ($e \downarrow$)
5. Appreciation worsens net exports ($NX \downarrow$)
6. The fall in NX **fully offsets** the fiscal expansion

**Result**: Fiscal policy is **completely ineffective** under floating rates with perfect capital mobility. Output returns to its original level.

```
Exchange Rate (e)
    │
    │      LM*
    │        │
    │        │  IS₁*  IS₂*
    │        │   /    /
    │        │  /    /
    └────────┼─/────/──→ Output (Y)
             Y* (unchanged)
             e₁  e₂ (appreciation: e↓)
```

**The crowding out mechanism**: Under floating rates, fiscal expansion is crowded out not by higher interest rates (which are pinned at $r^*$) but by exchange rate appreciation and the resulting collapse in net exports.

## Comparison: Floating vs. Fixed Exchange Rates

| Policy | Floating Rates | Fixed Rates |
|---|---|---|
| **Monetary policy** | Highly effective | Ineffective |
| **Fiscal policy** | Ineffective | Highly effective |

This stark reversal is one of the most celebrated results in open-economy macroeconomics and underlies the [[不可能三角]].

## Real-World Examples

### United States (Post-1971)
- Floats against major currencies (with varying degrees of management)
- Fed relies on monetary policy for stabilization
- Fiscal policy effects are partially offset by dollar appreciation (e.g., Reagan deficits 1980s)

### Japan (Post-1973)
- Yen floats, though with occasional intervention
- Bank of Japan uses monetary policy as primary tool
- Fiscal stimulus packages (1990s) had limited effect due to yen appreciation

### United Kingdom (Post-1992)
- Left the European Exchange Rate Mechanism (ERM) in 1992
- Bank of England gained operational independence in 1997
- Monetary policy became the primary stabilization tool

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 财政政策的"完全无效"过于极端

Mundell-Fleming预测浮动汇率下财政政策乘数为0,但现实中:
- **美国1980s**:里根财政扩张(减税+军费)确实伴随巨额贸易赤字,但产出明显增长——并非完全无效
- **日本1990s**:财政刺激效果有限,但部分原因是货币政策同时紧缩和结构性问题,而非纯粹的汇率挤出
- **不完全资本流动**:若资本流动不完全,利率可以偏离r*,财政政策部分有效

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 汇率传递的不完全性

Mundell-Fleming假设汇率变化完全传递至净出口,但实证:
- **汇率传递率**:进口价格对汇率变化的反应通常小于1(0.3-0.6)
- **定价 to market**:出口商按当地货币定价,吸收汇率波动
- ** invoicing currency**:美元作为全球 invoicing 货币,使非美国家汇率对贸易的影响减弱

这些摩擦削弱了汇率作为货币政策传导渠道的有效性。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] "害怕浮动"(fear of floating)

许多法定浮动汇率国家实际上大量干预:
- **中国**:声称"有管理的浮动",但汇率波动远小于纯浮动模型预测
- **印度**:RBI频繁干预外汇市场
- **拉美国家**:即使法定浮动,央行也积极管理汇率波动

Calvo-Reinhart (2002) 发现,宣称浮动的国家中,汇率波动率远低于真正浮动国家(如美国),利率波动率则更高——暗示它们实际上在"隐性固定"。

### 数据空白

1. **汇率制度分类的模糊性**:法定(de jure) vs 实际(de facto)制度差异巨大
2. **资本流动的突然停止**:与"完美流动"假设矛盾,2008后新兴市场多次经历资本外流
3. **全球安全资产需求**:美元作为全球储备货币使美国享有"过度特权", Mundell-Fleming 的 symmetric 分析不适用
