# 金融加速器

The **financial accelerator**, developed by [[Ben Bernanke]], Mark Gertler, and Simon Gilchrist (1999), formalizes how **credit market imperfections amplify economic fluctuations**. It explains why small shocks can cause large recessions and why recovery from financial crises is typically slow.

## The Core Mechanism

### External Finance Premium

With asymmetric information:
- **External finance** (borrowing) is more costly than **internal finance** (retained earnings)
- The premium depends on the borrower's **net worth** and **collateral**

$$\text{External finance cost} = \text{risk-free rate} + \phi(\text{net worth})$$

where $\phi' < 0$: higher net worth → lower premium.

### The Feedback Loop

1. **Negative shock** reduces asset prices and firm net worth
2. **Lower net worth** → higher external finance premium
3. **Higher premium** → reduced investment and employment
4. **Reduced activity** → lower output → further asset price declines
5. **Amplification**: The initial shock is magnified through the credit channel

$$\text{Small shock} \times \text{financial accelerator} = \text{Large recession}$$

## Formal Structure

Investment depends on both Tobin's q and net worth:

$$I = I(q, N), \quad \frac{\partial I}{\partial N} > 0$$

where $N$ is net worth. A decline in $N$ shifts the investment schedule inward.

Net worth is procyclical:
- Booms: Rising asset prices → higher net worth → easier credit → more investment → boom amplifies
- Recessions: Falling asset prices → lower net worth → tighter credit → less investment → recession deepens

## Application to the 2008 Crisis

The financial accelerator was the central mechanism of the Great Recession:

1. **Housing price collapse** (2006–2009): ~30% decline nationally
2. **Household net worth destruction**: $16 trillion lost
3. **Credit freeze**: Banks stopped lending as collateral values fell
4. **Business investment collapse**: Firms could not borrow to fund operations
5. **Unemployment surge**: 10% by late 2009
6. **Slow recovery**: Net worth rebuilds gradually; credit relationships take years to restore

## Extensions

### The "Risk-Taking Channel"

Borio-Zhu (2012): Low interest rates for extended periods encourage banks to take excessive risk ("search for yield"), building financial vulnerabilities that amplify future downturns.

### Household Financial Accelerator

For households:
- Home equity is the primary collateral for consumer borrowing
- Falling house prices reduce HELOC availability
- This directly affects consumption through the collateral channel

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **金融加速器是放大器而非源头**: BGG模型假设冲击来自实体经济(技术、需求)，金融部门只是放大。但2008危机显示金融部门自身可以产生冲击(投机→泡沫→崩溃)。[[Hyman Minsky]]的金融不稳定假说可能更适合解释内生金融危机。
- **QE的有效性争议**: 实证研究显示QE对长期利率的影响有限且短暂；主要通过"信号渠道"(改变预期)而非"资产组合再平衡"。但Bernanke认为QE防止了第二次大萧条——这一反事实无法验证。
- **分配后果被忽略**: QE推高资产价格，使富人(持有股票/房产)受益，而穷人(依赖工资/储蓄)受损。教材对金融危机政策的分配维度讨论不足。
- **微观基础不够微观**: BGG使用代表性代理人。异质性代理人模型显示：不同wealth群体的信贷约束差异巨大，aggregate响应不能由representative agent捕捉。
- **regional-bias**: 以美国Fed为中心；欧央行(Draghi "whatever it takes")、英国央行、日本央行(黑田东彦QQE)的危机应对差异巨大，教材未系统比较。
