# 政府购买乘数

The **government purchases multiplier** measures the change in equilibrium output resulting from a one-unit change in government spending. It is a central concept in Keynesian macroeconomics and a key input into fiscal policy design.

## The Basic Multiplier (Keynesian Cross)

In the [[凯恩斯交叉模型]], where prices and interest rates are fixed:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta G} = \frac{1}{1 - MPC}$$

For a typical marginal propensity to consume of $MPC = 0.75$:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta G} = \frac{1}{0.25} = 4$$

Each dollar of government spending generates four dollars of total output through successive rounds of re-spending.

## The Multiplier with Crowding Out (IS-LM)

In the [[IS-LM模型]], fiscal expansion raises output but also increases interest rates, which crowds out private investment:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta G} \bigg|_{IS-LM} < \frac{1}{1 - MPC}$$

The exact size depends on:
- **The slope of LM**: Steeper LM → larger interest rate increase → more crowding out → smaller multiplier
- **Interest sensitivity of investment**: More sensitive investment → more crowding out → smaller multiplier
- **The slope of IS**: Flatter IS → larger output response → larger multiplier

In extreme cases:
- **Liquidity trap** (horizontal LM): No crowding out, multiplier = $1/(1-MPC)$
- **Classical case** (vertical LM): Full crowding out, multiplier = 0

## The Open-Economy Multiplier

In the [[Mundell-Fleming模型]] (small open economy with perfect capital mobility):

**Floating exchange rates**: Fiscal multiplier = 0
- Government spending raises interest rates
- Capital inflows appreciate the exchange rate
- Net exports fall completely offsetting the fiscal expansion

**Fixed exchange rates**: Fiscal multiplier > 0 (potentially large)
- Central bank must expand money supply to prevent appreciation
- No crowding out via interest rates or exchange rates

## Empirical Estimates

Estimating the government purchases multiplier is one of the most contested empirical questions in macroeconomics:

| Study / Method | Estimate | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Simple Keynesian cross | 2–5 | Theoretical, fixed prices and interest rates |
| Ramey (2011), defense shocks | 0.6–1.2 | US historical data, VAR identification |
| Barro-Redlick (2011) | ~0.5 | War spending, cross-country panel |
| Auerbach-Gorodnichenko (2012) | 1.5–2.0 (recession); ~0 (expansion) | State-dependent multiplier |
| Blanchard-Leigh (2013) | IMF underestimated | European austerity, 2008 crisis |
| Nakamura-Steinsson (2014) | ~1.5 | Cross-state fiscal spillovers |

## The Multiplier and Economic Conditions

A growing consensus suggests the multiplier is **state-dependent**:

**Larger in recessions**:
- Slack resources (unemployed labor, idle capital) are brought into use
- Interest rates are low, less crowding out
- Monetary policy is accommodative
- Households are liquidity-constrained (high MPC)

**Smaller in expansions**:
- Resources are fully employed, fiscal expansion mainly raises prices
- Central bank may tighten to offset fiscal stimulus
- Households are less liquidity-constrained

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 乘数估计的识别难题

政府支出很少是外生的——它通常是对经济状况的反应(自动稳定器)或政治周期驱动。识别真正的"因果"乘数需要:
- **自然实验**:如Ramey使用的国防支出新闻(基于地缘政治事件)
- **叙事方法**:如Romer-Romer使用美联储政策会议记录
- **工具变量**:如Nakamura-Steinsson使用跨州军费支出差异

但每种方法都有争议:国防支出可能通过信心渠道影响经济;跨州差异可能存在溢出效应。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] Ricardian等价

Barro (1974)提出:如果消费者向前看且考虑代际预算约束,减税(或增加支出而不增税)不会改变消费——因为消费者预期未来增税。此时:
- 政府购买乘数 = 1(政府支出直接计入GDP,但私人消费下降完全抵消)
- 税收乘数 = 0

虽然实证上Ricardian等价不严格成立(部分家庭受信贷约束),但它对Keynesian乘数的规范结论构成重大理论挑战。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 供给侧效应被忽略

传统乘数分析假设政府支出是纯需求侧行为。但:
- **公共投资**(基础设施、教育、研发)可能提高长期生产率
- **政府消费**(公务员薪酬、转移支付)可能降低劳动供给激励
- **公共资本拥挤**:大型项目可能占用稀缺资源(工程师、建筑设备),推高私人部门成本

这些供给侧效应可能使净乘数与标准估计显著不同。

### 数据空白

1. **乘数的异质性**:基础设施乘数 vs 转移支付乘数 vs 减税乘数差异巨大,但教材使用笼统的"政府购买乘数"
2. **时间轮廓**:冲击的效应随时间衰减——但教材使用"一次性"乘数
3. **分配效应**:政府支出对不同收入群体的效应差异巨大,但加总分析忽略这一点
4. **中国数据**:中国"四万亿"刺激(2008)的乘数估计在0.5-2.0之间,但受地方政府融资平台和房地产周期复杂影响
