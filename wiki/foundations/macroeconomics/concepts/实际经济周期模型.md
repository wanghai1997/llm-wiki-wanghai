# 实际经济周期模型

The **Real Business Cycle (RBC) model**, developed by [[Finn Kydland]] and [[Edward Prescott]] (1982), is the first modern macroeconomic model built entirely on explicit microfoundations—optimizing representative agents, rational expectations, and competitive markets. Its central claim: **business cycles are driven by real (technology) shocks, not monetary or demand factors**.

## Core Structure

### Representative Agent
A single consumer-worker maximizes expected lifetime utility:

$$E_0 \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \beta^t U(C_t, L_t)$$

subject to resource and technology constraints.

### Technology Shock
Output is produced via:

$$Y_t = A_t F(K_t, L_t)$$

where $A_t$ follows a stochastic process (typically an AR(1) in logs):

$$\ln A_t = \rho \ln A_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$$

The Solow residual serves as the empirical proxy for $A_t$.

### Market Clearing
- Prices and wages adjust instantaneously
- No nominal rigidities
- No unemployment (everyone who wants to work at the equilibrium wage is employed)

### Calibration, Not Estimation
Parameters are "calibrated" to match long-run growth facts:
- Capital share ≈ 1/3
- Investment/GDP ≈ 1/4
- Average growth ≈ 2%
- Average return to capital ≈ 6–7%

## The Propagation Mechanism

A positive technology shock:
1. Raises productivity → higher real wages
2. Higher wages → more labor supply (intertemporal substitution)
3. More labor → higher output
4. Higher output → more investment (to build capital for future production)
5. Boom persists through capital accumulation dynamics

## Prescott's Claim: 70% of Fluctuations Explained

Prescott argued the calibrated RBC model could explain **~70% of US output fluctuations** using technology shocks alone. This was deeply controversial.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 无法解释失业

RBC assumes continuous market clearing—there is no unemployment concept. Workers simply "choose" to work less during recessions. This is empirically implausible: involuntary unemployment rises sharply in recessions, and job finding rates fall (Davis-Haltiwanger 1992).

### [CONTRADICTION-2] Solow残差的内生性

The Solow residual is a **measurement** of unexplained productivity, not necessarily an exogenous shock. It includes:
- Measurement error in capital utilization
- Variable factor effort (workers work harder in booms)
- Demand shocks (firm-level demand affects measured TFP)
- Correction for variable utilization (Burnside-Eichenbaum-Rebelo 1993) reduces explanatory power to ~30–50%

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 消费的过度波动预测

RBC predicts consumption should be **more volatile** than output (since technology shocks are permanent, consumers want to front-load consumption). Empirically, consumption is **smoother** than output—the opposite prediction.

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 劳动供给弹性要求过高

RBC requires implausibly high intertemporal elasticity of labor supply (~3–4) to generate observed output fluctuations from modest wage changes. Micro estimates suggest elasticity closer to 0.5 (Chetty et al. 2011).

### [CONTRADICTION-5] 2008后的边缘化

RBC在2008金融危机后被急剧边缘化。金融摩擦、信贷中断、名义刚性被证明是理解波动的关键——而RBC全部排除。主流央行模型已全部转向新凯恩斯DSGE。

### 数据空白

1. **技术冲击的识别**: 如何区分真正的技术冲击与需求驱动的TFP变化？仍未解决。
2. **金融周期**: RBC框架无法纳入信贷周期、资产泡沫、银行危机——2008证明这些是核心。
3. **异质性代理人**: 代表性代理人假设忽略了不同收入/财富群体的差异化响应。HANK模型显示aggregate结果截然不同。
