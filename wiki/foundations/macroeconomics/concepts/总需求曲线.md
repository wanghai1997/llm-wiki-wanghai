# 总需求曲线

The **aggregate demand (AD) curve** shows the negative relationship between the overall price level ($P$) and the total quantity of goods and services demanded ($Y$) in the economy. It slopes downward for three distinct reasons.

## Why the AD Curve Slopes Downward

### 1. The Wealth Effect (Pigou Effect)

A lower price level increases the real value of households' money holdings. Feeling wealthier, consumers increase spending:

$$P \downarrow \Rightarrow \text{real wealth} \uparrow \Rightarrow C \uparrow$$

Named after Arthur Pigou (1943), this effect is sometimes called the **real balance effect**.

### 2. The Interest-Rate Effect (Keynes Effect)

A lower price level reduces the demand for money (since fewer dollars are needed for transactions). With a fixed money supply, the interest rate falls, stimulating investment:

$$P \downarrow \Rightarrow (M/P) \uparrow \Rightarrow r \downarrow \Rightarrow I \uparrow$$

This is the most important channel in Mankiw's exposition and the link between the AD curve and the [[IS-LM模型]].

### 3. The Exchange-Rate Effect (Mundell-Fleming Effect)

A lower domestic price level causes the real exchange rate to depreciate, making domestic goods cheaper relative to foreign goods, increasing net exports:

$$P \downarrow \Rightarrow \varepsilon \downarrow \Rightarrow NX \uparrow$$

This channel becomes central in the [[Mundell-Fleming模型]] (Ch 13).

## The AD Equation

In its most general form, aggregate demand is the sum of the four components of GDP:

$$Y = C(Y - T) + I(r) + G + NX(\varepsilon)$$

Any change in the underlying determinants—consumption, investment, government purchases, net exports, taxes, or the money supply—shifts the AD curve.

## Shifts in the AD Curve

| Shift Factor | Direction | Effect on AD |
|---|---|---|
| Increase in consumer confidence | Rightward | Higher $C$ at any price level |
| Expansionary fiscal policy ($G \uparrow$ or $T \downarrow$) | Rightward | Higher spending |
| Expansionary monetary policy ($M \uparrow$) | Rightward | Lower $r$ at any price level |
| Increase in net exports (foreign boom) | Rightward | Higher $NX$ |
| Stock market crash / wealth destruction | Leftward | Lower $C$ |
| Contractionary policy | Leftward | Lower spending |

## From IS-LM to AD

In Ch 12, Mankiw derives the AD curve explicitly from the [[IS-LM模型]]:
- For a given price level $P$, the real money supply $M/P$ determines the position of the LM curve
- The IS-LM intersection gives the equilibrium output $Y$
- Varying $P$ traces out the downward-sloping AD curve

This derivation makes explicit the connection between micro-level behavior (consumption, investment, money demand) and the macro-level AD curve.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] AD曲线的简化与复杂现实

AD曲线将总需求简化为价格水平与产出的二维关系,但现实中:
- **收入分配效应**:价格变化对不同收入群体的实际购买力影响不同,可能同时产生正负财富效应
- **债务-通缩机制**:Fisher (1933) 指出价格下降会增加实际债务负担,反而抑制消费和投资——与 Pigou 财富效应方向相反
- **预期渠道**:现代宏观强调预期驱动的AD波动(如前瞻指引、信心冲击),价格水平本身可能不是主要驱动力

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 货币政策传导渠道的现代扩展

教材将货币政策传导简化为 $M \uparrow \Rightarrow r \downarrow \Rightarrow I \uparrow$。但2008后:
- **零利率下限**:传统利率渠道失效
- **QE和资产组合再平衡**:央行购买长期资产压低期限溢价,不经过短期利率
- **信贷渠道**:银行资产负债表状况成为传导的关键摩擦
- **信号渠道**:政策行动改变市场预期,独立于当前利率

这些现代渠道无法被传统的AD曲线充分捕捉。

### 数据空白

1. **AD冲击的识别**:如何区分需求冲击 vs 供给冲击?实际数据中两者往往同时发生(如2020疫情)
2. **乘数的时变性**:财政乘数在衰退期 vs 扩张期差异显著(Auerbach-Gorodnichenko 2012),AD曲线应"会移动且会变形"
3. **全球AD**:在高度开放的经济体中,国内AD increasingly 由全球而非国内因素驱动
