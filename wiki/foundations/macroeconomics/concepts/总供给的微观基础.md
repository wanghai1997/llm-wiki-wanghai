# 总供给的微观基础

The **microfoundations of aggregate supply** refer to the theories that explain why the short-run aggregate supply (SRAS) curve slopes upward—why output responds to changes in the price level in the short run. Mankiw 9e Ch 14-1 presents three distinct explanations, each grounded in different assumptions about how markets and information work.

## The Three Theories

### 1. Sticky Wages

**Mechanism**: Nominal wages are set by long-term contracts and cannot adjust immediately. When the price level rises unexpectedly, real wages fall, making labor cheaper and increasing employment and output.

**Key assumptions**:
- Long-term nominal wage contracts
- Firms are on their labor demand curves (adjust employment to equate $W/P$ with marginal product)
- Workers are off their labor supply curves (supply whatever labor firms demand at the contracted wage)

**See**: [[粘性工资理论]]

### 2. Sticky Prices

**Mechanism**: Firms face menu costs when changing prices. When aggregate demand increases, only some firms adjust prices upward; those that don't experience higher relative demand and increase production.

**Key assumptions**:
- Menu costs (physical, decision, customer, coordination costs)
- Firms are price-setters (monopolistic competition)
- Staggered or random price adjustment

**See**: [[粘性价格理论]]

### 3. Imperfect Information

**Mechanism**: Producers confuse changes in the overall price level with changes in their own product's relative price. A general price increase is partially interpreted as a favorable relative demand shift, leading to increased production.

**Key assumptions**:
- Producers observe their own price but not the aggregate price level perfectly
- Signal extraction problem: separating aggregate from relative shocks
- Rational expectations about the distributions of shocks

**See**: [[不完全信息理论]]

## Comparison of the Three Theories

| Dimension | Sticky Wages | Sticky Prices | Imperfect Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominal rigidity** | Yes (wages) | Yes (prices) | No |
| **Key friction** | Contractual wage setting | Menu costs | Information asymmetry |
| **Price flexibility** | Prices flexible, wages sticky | Prices sticky | All prices flexible |
| **Employment** | Off labor supply curve | On labor supply curve | On labor supply curve |
| **Expectations** | Adaptive or static | Forward-looking (Calvo) | Rational expectations |
| **Policy ineffectiveness** | Temporary real effects | Temporary real effects | Only unanticipated policy matters |
| **Microfoundation** | Contract theory, efficiency wages | Menu cost models, Calvo pricing | Signal extraction, rational expectations |

## From Theories to the SRAS Curve

All three theories deliver the same reduced-form equation:

$$Y = \bar{Y} + \alpha(P - P^e)$$

or in terms of inflation:

$$\pi = \pi^e - \beta(u - u^n)$$

This is the **expectations-augmented Phillips curve**—the modern, post-Friedman-Phelps version of the relationship between inflation and unemployment.

## The New Keynesian Synthesis

Modern macroeconomics (New Keynesian DSGE models) primarily relies on **sticky prices** (Calvo pricing) as the microfoundation for aggregate supply:
- Most tractable for dynamic analysis
- Consistent with micro evidence on price adjustment
- Delivers the forward-looking New Keynesian Phillips curve:

$$\pi_t = \beta E_t\pi_{t+1} + \kappa (y_t - \bar{y}_t)$$

This equation is the workhorse of modern central bank models.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 三种理论互不兼容,教材却并列呈现

教材将三种理论并列,暗示它们是"互补解释",但:
- **Sticky wages** 假设价格灵活、工资粘性
- **Sticky prices** 假设工资灵活、价格粘性
- **Imperfect information** 假设两者都灵活

这三种机制不可能同时是SRAS向上倾斜的主要原因。现实可能是:
- 不同行业、不同时间主导机制不同
- 某些行业工资合同重要(制造业),某些行业价格粘性重要(服务业)
- 2008危机主要是金融摩擦(未在三种理论中出现)

教材未讨论各机制的**相对重要性**和**适用条件**。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 金融摩擦的完全缺失

三种微基础都忽略了**金融部门**:
- **Bernanke-Gertler-Gilchrist (1999)**:信贷市场摩擦放大经济波动
- **2008危机**:银行危机→信贷冻结→企业无法融资→产出崩溃——与价格/工资粘性无关
- **金融加速器**:资产价格下跌→企业净值下降→外部融资成本上升→投资下降

这是教材最重要的知识缺口之一。现代宏观已将金融摩擦视为与价格粘性同等重要的波动来源。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 微观基础 vs 宏观拟合的张力

新凯恩斯主义以"微基础"自豪,但:
- Calvo定价的"随机调整概率"假设在现实中不存在
- 标准NKPC参数(价格持续期、边际成本弹性)的估计不稳定
- 模型为了拟合数据需要引入大量"ad hoc"冲击(成本推动、需求、偏好、风险溢价)

"微基础"可能更多是方法论修辞,而非真正的理论进步。

### 数据空白

1. **各机制的贡献分解**:如何量化粘性工资 vs 粘性价格 vs 不完全信息对实际波动的贡献?缺乏系统识别方法
2. **跨部门异质性**:制造业(工会密集) vs 服务业(技术密集)的价格/工资调整机制截然不同
3. **金融摩擦的量化**:信贷利差、杠杆周期、银行健康度如何进入SRAS?
4. **行为因素**:参照依赖、损失厌恶、有限注意力如何影响价格设定?
