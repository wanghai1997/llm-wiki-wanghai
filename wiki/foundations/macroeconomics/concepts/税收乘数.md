# 税收乘数

The **tax multiplier** measures the change in equilibrium output resulting from a one-unit change in taxes. Unlike the government purchases multiplier, only a fraction of a tax cut is spent—the rest is saved—making the tax multiplier smaller in absolute value.

## The Basic Tax Multiplier (Keynesian Cross)

In the [[凯恩斯交叉模型]]:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta T} = -\frac{MPC}{1 - MPC}$$

For $MPC = 0.75$:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta T} = -\frac{0.75}{0.25} = -3$$

A tax cut of $1 increases output by $3. The negative sign reflects that a tax increase reduces disposable income and consumption.

## Comparison: Spending vs. Tax Multiplier

| | Government Purchases | Tax Cut |
|---|---|---|
| Initial round | $\Delta G$ directly adds to PE | $\Delta T$ increases disposable income by $\Delta T$ |
| First induced spending | $MPC \times \Delta G$ | $MPC \times \Delta T$ |
| Full multiplier | $\frac{1}{1-MPC}$ | $\frac{MPC}{1-MPC}$ |
| For MPC = 0.75 | 4 | 3 |

**Key insight**: Government purchases have a larger multiplier than tax cuts because the initial round of spending enters directly into aggregate demand. A tax cut must first pass through household saving before affecting demand.

## The Balanced Budget Multiplier

If the government increases both spending and taxes by the same amount ($\Delta G = \Delta T$):

$$\Delta Y = \underbrace{\frac{1}{1-MPC} \Delta G}_{\text{spending effect}} + \underbrace{\left(-\frac{MPC}{1-MPC}\right) \Delta T}_{\text{tax effect}}$$

Since $\Delta G = \Delta T$:

$$\Delta Y = \frac{1 - MPC}{1 - MPC} \Delta G = \Delta G$$

**The balanced budget multiplier equals 1.** A deficit-neutral fiscal expansion still stimulates output because the direct government spending effect outweighs the indirect tax effect.

## Tax Multiplier with Crowding Out (IS-LM)

In the [[IS-LM模型]], the tax multiplier is further reduced by crowding out:

$$\left|\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta T}\right|_{IS-LM} < \frac{MPC}{1-MPC}$$

The tax cut raises disposable income and consumption, shifting IS right. But higher output increases money demand, raising interest rates and crowding out investment.

## The Open-Economy Tax Multiplier

In the [[Mundell-Fleming模型]]:

**Floating exchange rates**: Tax multiplier ≈ 0 (small, possibly negative)
- Tax cut raises consumption and imports
- Capital inflows appreciate the currency
- Net exports fall, offsetting the demand increase

**Fixed exchange rates**: Tax multiplier > 0
- Central bank accommodates the demand increase
- No crowding out via interest rates or exchange rates

## Types of Taxes and Their Multipliers

Not all taxes have the same multiplier:
- **Lump-sum taxes** (analyzed above): Simplest case
- **Proportional income taxes**: Reduce the multiplier because part of any income change is automatically taxed away. The multiplier becomes $\frac{1}{1 - MPC(1-t)}$ where $t$ is the tax rate.
- **Payroll taxes**: Directly affect labor costs and employment
- **Corporate taxes**: Affect investment incentives
- **Transfer payments** (negative taxes): Unemployment benefits, stimulus checks—high multiplier because recipients are liquidity-constrained

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 税收乘数的实证微弱性

实证研究表明税收乘数可能大于理论预测:
- **Romer-Romer (2010)**:美国税收变化的乘数约为 -3(增税1%GDP,产出下降3%)——远大于简单理论
- **原因**:税收变化可能通过信心渠道、不确定性、信贷约束等机制放大效应
- **异质性**:对高收入群体的减税乘数接近0(低MPC),对低收入群体的减税乘数接近2(高MPC)

教材使用统一税收乘数,未呈现这种异质性。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 供给侧税收效应被忽略

传统税收乘数只考虑需求侧效应,但税收也有供给侧影响:
- **劳动所得税**:降低边际税率可能增加劳动供给(Laffer曲线效应)
- **资本所得税**:影响储蓄和投资决策
- **企业税**:影响选址、创新和竞争力

Reagan减税(1981)和Trump减税(2017)的支持者强调供给侧效应,但教材几乎完全忽略。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 自动稳定器的教材处理不足

教材将税收作为政策工具,但现实中大部分税收变化是**自动稳定器**:
- 累进所得税自动在衰退期减税、在繁荣期增税
- 失业救济金自动在衰退期增加
- 这些"被动"财政政策的稳定效果可能比"主动"刺激更可靠

但教材对自动稳定器的讨论远少于对 discretionary fiscal policy 的讨论。

### 数据空白

1. **税收变化的分配效应**:减税对不同收入群体的消费影响差异巨大
2. **预期效应**:预期未来增税可能抵消当前减税的刺激效果
3. **债务可持续性**:持续减税增加政府债务,可能引发未来的财政紧缩——动态税收乘数
4. **国际税收竞争**:企业税减税可能引发各国竞争性减税,净效应复杂
