# 新凯恩斯主义模型

The **New Keynesian model** combines the microfoundations of RBC with **nominal rigidities** (sticky prices and wages), yielding a framework where monetary policy has real effects and stabilization policy is welfare-improving. It is the dominant paradigm for modern central bank forecasting and policy analysis.

## The Three Equations

The canonical New Keynesian model consists of three core equations:

### 1. Dynamic IS Curve (Euler Equation)

$$y_t = E_t[y_{t+1}] - \sigma^{-1}(i_t - E_t[\pi_{t+1}] - r_t^n)$$

Current output depends on expected future output and the real interest rate gap.

### 2. New Keynesian Phillips Curve

$$\pi_t = \beta E_t[\pi_{t+1}] + \kappa(y_t - y_t^n)$$

Inflation depends on expected future inflation and the output gap. This is a forward-looking, microfounded Phillips curve derived from Calvo (1983) pricing.

### 3. Taylor Rule (Monetary Policy)

$$i_t = r_t^n + \phi_\pi \pi_t + \phi_y(y_t - y_t^n)$$

The central bank responds to inflation and the output gap.

## Microfoundations: The Calvo Pricing Model

[[Guillermo Calvo]] (1983): Each period, a random fraction $(1-\theta)$ of firms can reset prices; the remaining $\theta$ keep prices fixed.

- Higher $\theta$ → more price stickiness → larger real effects of monetary shocks
- Typical calibration: $\theta \approx 0.75$ (prices fixed for ~4 quarters on average)

This generates the **New Keynesian Phillips curve** from firm profit maximization.

## From RBC to NK: What Changes

| Feature | RBC | New Keynesian |
|---|---|---|
| Shocks | Technology only | Technology + monetary + demand |
| Nominal rigidities | None | Price/wage stickiness |
| Monetary policy | Neutral | Has real effects |
| Unemployment | None | Search/matching or wage rigidity |
| Policy recommendation | "Do nothing" | Active stabilization |

## DSGE as Policy Tool

Central banks use estimated New Keynesian DSGE models for:
- **Forecasting**: Conditional projections of inflation, output, interest rates
- **Policy simulation**: Counterfactual analysis of alternative interest rate paths
- **Optimal policy**: Deriving Ramsey-optimal monetary policy rules

Examples: Federal Reserve's FRB/US, ECB's New Area-Wide Model, Bank of England's COMPASS.

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **单一代表性代理人**: 标准NK模型假设同质家庭，忽略了收入分配对aggregate demand的影响。HANK模型(Kaplan-Moll-Violante 2018)发现：不同wealth群体的MPC差异巨大，aggregate MPC与representative agent模型预测截然不同。
- **金融部门的缺失**: 标准NK模型不包含金融中介、信贷摩擦或资产负债表渠道。2008危机表明金融部门是波动核心——这催生了"金融加速器"+NK的混合模型(Christiano-Eichenbaum-Trabandt 2015)。
- **零利率下限(ZLB)的困难**: 当 $i = 0$ 时，标准Taylor规则失效。前瞻性指引、QE、负利率等工具超出了三方程框架。
- **估计的挑战**: DSGE模型的估计依赖强先验（Bayesian），且模型选择("哪些冲击？哪些摩擦？")很大程度上是ad hoc的。Canova-Sala (2009) 证明不同DSGE模型对同一数据的拟合可能给出矛盾的政策建议。
- **regional-bias**: NK模型以美国/欧洲为校准基准。发展中国家(制度不稳定、金融不发达、外部冲击主导)中，名义刚性可能不是主要摩擦——汇率波动、资本流动、大宗商品价格可能更重要。
