# 平衡增长路径

A **balanced growth path (BGP)** is a trajectory along which all key macroeconomic variables grow at constant (though possibly different) rates, with stable ratios between them. It is the central prediction of the augmented [[Solow增长模型]] with technological progress, and it corresponds closely to the empirical regularities documented by [[Nicholas Kaldor]] as the "stylized facts" of growth.

## Kaldor's Stylized Facts (1961)

In his 1961 paper, Kaldor summarized six empirical regularities that growth theory should explain:

1. Per-capita output grows over time, and its growth rate does not tend to diminish
2. Physical capital per worker grows over time
3. The rate of return on capital is roughly stable
4. The capital-output ratio is roughly stable
5. The shares of labor and capital in national income are roughly stable
6. The growth rate of output per worker differs substantially across countries

## The Solow Model's Reconciliation

The Solow model with labor-augmenting technological progress ($Y = F(K, L \cdot E)$, $\dot{E}/E = g$) predicts that the economy converges to a BGP with the following properties:

### Growth Rates on the BGP

| Variable | Symbol | Growth Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Output per effective worker | $\tilde{y} = Y/(LE)$ | 0 |
| Capital per effective worker | $\tilde{k} = K/(LE)$ | 0 |
| Output per worker | $y = Y/L$ | $g$ |
| Capital per worker | $k = K/L$ | $g$ |
| Total output | $Y$ | $n + g$ |
| Total capital | $K$ | $n + g$ |
| Wage rate | $w = \partial Y/\partial L$ | $g$ |
| Rental rate of capital | $R = \partial Y/\partial K$ | 0 |

### Stable Ratios

On the BGP:
- $K/Y = \tilde{k}^*/f(\tilde{k}^*)$ = constant
- $K/L$ grows at rate $g$
- $Y/L$ grows at rate $g$
- Labor share = $wL/Y = 1 - \alpha_K$ (constant for Cobb-Douglas)
- Capital share = $RK/Y = \alpha_K$ (constant)

Thus the model successfully explains Kaldor's facts 1–5. Fact 6 (cross-country differences in growth rates) is addressed by **conditional convergence** (see [[收敛假说]]) and the [[Mankiw-Romer-Weil模型]].

## Why "Balanced"?

Growth is "balanced" because:
- Capital and effective labor grow at the same rate ($n + g$), so their ratio stays constant
- Output grows at the same rate as inputs, so output per unit of input is constant
- Factor prices adjust to keep income shares stable

This is not a knife-edge result: the Solow model's steady state is globally stable, so the economy converges to the BGP from any initial capital stock.

## Deviations from Balanced Growth

Real economies deviate from the BGP in several ways:

1. **Transitional dynamics**: Countries far from their steady state grow faster or slower than $g$
2. **Business cycles**: Short-run fluctuations around the trend due to demand shocks
3. **Structural change**: Sectoral shifts (agriculture → manufacturing → services) change factor intensities and measured TFP
4. **Technology waves**: General-purpose technologies (steam, electricity, computers, AI) may cause temporary accelerations or decelerations in measured productivity

## In Mankiw's Textbook

Mankiw Ch 9-1 derives the BGP properties from the Solow model with technological progress. Ch 9-2 notes the correspondence with Kaldor's stylized facts, presenting it as empirical validation of the model.

The textbook does not explore:
- **Why** technological progress takes the labor-augmenting form (Uzawa 1961 theorem)
- Whether BGP is a good approximation for developing countries undergoing structural transformation
- The relationship between BGP and business cycle fluctuations

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION] 结构转型 vs 平衡增长
Kaldor 的 stylized facts 主要基于发达经济体（美国、英国）20 世纪的数据。对于正在经历结构转型的经济体（农业→工业→服务业），BGP 是一个较差的近似：
- 资本密集度在不同部门间差异巨大
- 劳动力从低生产率部门向高生产率部门的转移本身贡献了增长（这种 "structural bonus" 被计入 TFP，但不代表真正的技术进步）
- 中国 1978-2015 的增长很大一部分来自资源重新配置（农业到制造业），而非纯粹的 BGP 动态

### [BIAS] 发达国家经验的一般化
教材将 Kaldor facts（主要来自英美经验）作为所有经济体应遵循的"自然规律"。但：
- 东亚经济体在追赶阶段经历了资本-产出比的显著变化
- 石油出口国的资本-产出比受资源价格波动影响
- 数字经济的崛起可能正在改变要素份额（资本份额在 1980s 后上升，挑战了"稳定要素份额"假说）

### 数据空白
- **资本份额上升之谜**：Piketty (2014) 和 Karabarbounis-Neiman (2014) 记录全球劳动份额下降、资本份额上升，与 Kaldor fact 5 矛盾。这是否意味着 BGP 假设失效，或只是过渡现象？
- **无形资产与测度**：BGP 框架假设资本和产出可被准确测度。但无形资本（软件、R&D、品牌）的崛起使 $K/Y$ 比率难以解释
- **零利率下限与 BGP**：在 BGP 上，$MPK - \delta = n + g$。如果 $n + g$ 下降（如发达经济体的人口老龄化和生产率减速），BGP 要求更低的资本回报率。这与"secular stagnation"假说相关
