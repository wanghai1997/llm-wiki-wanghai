# 不可能三角

The **impossible trinity** (also called the **policy trilemma** or **Mundell-Fleming trilemma**) states that a country cannot simultaneously maintain all three of the following:

1. **Free capital mobility** (no capital controls)
2. **A fixed exchange rate** (pegged or managed)
3. **An independent monetary policy** (domestic interest rate targeting)

A country must choose **two** of the three. The choice of which two to pursue determines the exchange rate regime and macroeconomic policy framework.

## The Three Corners of the Triangle

```
         Free Capital Mobility
                /\
               /  \
              /    \
             /      \
            /   ???  \
           /__________\
Fixed Exchange Rate    Independent
                       Monetary Policy
```

### Option 1: Free Capital + Fixed Exchange Rate → No Independent Monetary Policy

**Examples**:
- Hong Kong (currency board pegged to USD)
- Argentina 1991–2001 (currency board)
- Eurozone members (irrevocably fixed against each other)

**Characteristics**:
- Domestic interest rate tracks the anchor country's rate
- Monetary policy is imported
- Fiscal policy becomes the primary stabilization tool
- Vulnerable to speculative attacks if fiscal sustainability is questioned

### Option 2: Free Capital + Independent Monetary Policy → Floating Exchange Rate

**Examples**:
- United States
- United Kingdom
- Japan
- Australia

**Characteristics**:
- Central bank sets domestic interest rate freely
- Exchange rate adjusts to equilibrate capital flows
- Monetary policy is the primary stabilization tool
- Fiscal policy partially crowded out by exchange rate movements

### Option 3: Fixed Exchange Rate + Independent Monetary Policy → Capital Controls

**Examples**:
- China (managed float with capital controls)
- India (capital controls with flexible rate)
- Malaysia 1997–1998 (temporary capital controls during Asian crisis)

**Characteristics**:
- Central bank can set domestic interest rate independently
- Exchange rate is managed
- Capital controls insulate from international financial flows
- Controls may be evaded, create distortions, and are difficult to enforce

## Historical Evolution of the Trilemma

### The Bretton Woods Era (1944–1971)
- **Choice**: Fixed rates + capital controls → limited monetary independence
- Capital mobility was low due to wartime destruction and explicit controls
- Countries had modest monetary policy autonomy within the adjustable peg system

### The Post-Bretton Woods Era (1971–present)
- Capital mobility increased dramatically
- Most advanced economies moved to **Option 2** (float + independent monetary policy)
- Some emerging markets tried **Option 1** (currency boards, dollarization) with mixed results
- China has pursued **Option 3** (managed rate + controls) while gradually liberalizing

### The Eurozone
- The euro represents an extreme form of **Option 1**: irrevocably fixed rates + free capital + ECB monetary policy
- Individual member states have **no** monetary policy independence
- The euro crisis (2010–2015) revealed the costs of this choice for asymmetric shocks

## The Trilemma in Practice

### "Fear of Floating"

Many countries that claim to float actually intervene heavily (Calvo and Reinhart 2002):
- They want monetary independence (Option 2)
- But they fear exchange rate volatility
- Result: "Dirty floating" or "managed floating"—a partial abandonment of the float

### The Rise of Macroprudential Policies

Some economists argue that the trilemma can be softened with **macroprudential policies**:
- Capital flow management measures (CFMs) as a "third tool"
- Financial transaction taxes
- Loan-to-value ratios and reserve requirements
- These are not full capital controls but may provide partial insulation

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 三元悖论过于简化

三元悖论是一个有力的概念框架,但现实中:
- **不完全资本流动**:大多数国家介于"完全自由"和"完全管制"之间
- **不完全汇率固定**:爬行钉住、篮子货币、区间浮动等"中间制度"普遍存在
- **不完全货币独立**:即使浮动汇率国家,其利率也受全球金融周期影响(Rey 2015)

Rey (2015) 提出"二元悖论"(dilemma, not trilemma):在资本自由流动条件下,即使浮动汇率也无法保证货币政策独立,因为全球金融周期主导一切。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 美元霸权使美国豁免

美国作为全球储备货币发行国,在一定程度上"绕过"三元悖论:
- 资本自由流动 ✓
- 美元汇率浮动(对其他主要货币) ✓
- 货币政策独立 ✓
- 同时享受:全球对安全资产的需求使美国可以大量发债而不推高利率
- 货币政策通过全球美元流动性渠道影响全球,而非仅国内

这是"过度特权"(exorbitant privilege),使 Mundell-Fleming 的对称分析对美国不适用。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 数字货币可能重构三难困境

CBDC(央行数字货币)的引入可能:
- 使资本管制更有效(可编程货币)
- 改变跨境支付和储备货币格局
- 创造新的"数字美元化"风险

三元悖论在数字货币时代的适用性尚不明确,教材完全未涉及。

### 数据空白

1. **中间汇率制度的绩效**:法定分类 vs 实际行为的差异——"害怕浮动"国家的宏观经济表现
2. **宏观审慎作为第三工具**:CFM在多大程度上可以缓解三元悖论?IMF 2012年后态度转变支持CFM
3. **全球金融周期**:Rey (2015) 的证据表明,资本流动使各国货币政策 increasingly 同步
4. **数字货币与货币主权**:CBDC、稳定币、比特币对汇率制度和货币政策独立性的挑战

### [regional-bias] 中国变体:三选二的"中间态"

按 [[孙国峰]] 《健全现代货币政策框架》(2021) 与 PBoC 实践,中国不可能三角的实际选择**不是教科书的"三选二"清晰角点**,而是**三个目标都部分实现**:

- **货币政策独立性**:中国通过 [[OMO公开市场操作-中国|OMO]] / [[MLF中期借贷便利|MLF]] / 准备金率独立设定国内利率,但又受全球美元利率周期影响。
- **管理浮动**:[[人民币汇率市场化]] 改革后人民币对美元有浮动空间,但 PBoC 通过逆周期调节因子 + 中间价管理保留干预选项。
- **资本部分流动**:2015 后陆股通 / 债券通 / 跨境理财通逐步开放,但保留必要的跨境资本流动审慎管理(如离岸人民币 CNH 池调节)。

中国版"三选二"的实证表现:

1. **2015 "8.11"汇改**:汇率市场化导致大规模资本外流(每月约 1000 亿美元),被迫加强资本管制 → 验证三元悖论。
2. **2019 "破 7"**:汇率突破 7.0 后,资本管制 + 美元 / CNH 流动性管理使外流可控 → "管理浮动 + 资本部分流动"组合成功。
3. **2024 一篮子货币 + CFETS 指数**:汇率管理重点从对美元到一篮子,**减少**对美元利率周期的强制对应。

**理论意义**:中国实践支持 Rey 2015 "二元悖论"——但通过 CFM 缓解"全球金融周期"约束。Mundell-Fleming 教科书的"小型开放经济"清晰二选一不适用大型经济体。
