# 随机游走假说

The **random walk hypothesis of consumption**, developed by [[Robert Hall]] (1978), combines the [[永久收入假说]] with [[理性预期]] to derive a striking prediction: **changes in consumption should be unpredictable**. Only unanticipated changes in lifetime resources should affect consumption; any information that was already expected is already incorporated into current consumption.

## Derivation

### The Euler Equation

From the Fisher intertemporal optimization framework, the first-order condition for optimal consumption is:

$$U'(C_t) = \frac{1}{1+\rho} E_t[(1+r) U'(C_{t+1})]$$

With quadratic utility (or log utility with specific assumptions) and $r = \rho$:

$$E_t[C_{t+1}] = C_t$$

### The Random Walk Result

This implies:

$$C_{t+1} = C_t + \varepsilon_{t+1}$$

where $\varepsilon_{t+1}$ is a **forecast error** that is:
- Unpredictable at time $t$: $E_t[\varepsilon_{t+1}] = 0$
- Uncorrelated with any information available at time $t$

**In words**: The best forecast of next period's consumption is this period's consumption. Nothing observable today helps predict how consumption will change tomorrow.

## Policy Implications

### Systematic Policy is Ineffective

The random walk hypothesis has profound implications for stabilization policy:

- **Anticipated tax changes**: If the government announces a tax cut to take effect next year, rational consumers adjust consumption **today**, not when the tax cut arrives. By the time the policy is implemented, it has no effect on consumption.
- **Unanticipated changes**: Only surprise policy changes affect consumption. But surprises cannot be part of a systematic policy rule.
- **Conclusion**: Attempts to "fine-tune" consumption through predictable fiscal policy are futile under rational expectations.

This is a consumption-sector application of the broader **policy ineffectiveness proposition** associated with Lucas and Sargent.

### The Connection to Lucas Critique

Hall's result is a direct consequence of the [[Lucas批判]]: if policymakers try to exploit the consumption-income relationship estimated from past data, consumers' rational expectations will change their behavior, making the historical relationship useless for policy evaluation.

## Empirical Testing

### Hall's Original Tests

Hall (1978) tested whether lagged variables (income, stock prices, consumption lags) predict consumption changes. His findings:
- **Consumption lags**: Do not predict future consumption changes (consistent with random walk)
- **Stock prices**: Do predict consumption changes (because they contain new information about lifetime wealth)
- **Lagged income**: Does not predict consumption changes (inconsistent with Keynesian consumption function)

### Subsequent Evidence

**Supporting the random walk**:
- Flavin (1981): Confirmed Hall's basic result but found "excess sensitivity" to predictable income changes
- Campbell-Mankiw (1989): Estimated that ~50% of consumers follow the random walk, while ~50% follow a rule-of-thumb (current income)

**Challenging the random walk**:
- **Excess sensitivity** (Flavin 1981): Consumption responds to **predictable** income changes more than the model allows — suggests liquidity constraints or myopia
- **Excess smoothness** (Campbell-Deaton 1989): Consumption is **too smooth** relative to income — suggests incomplete information or habit formation
- **Habit formation**: Carroll et al. (2000), Fuhrer (2000): past consumption affects current utility, creating inertia not captured by the random walk

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 过度敏感:流动性约束还是非理性?

Flavin (1981)发现消费对可预测的收入变化过于敏感。两种解释:
- **流动性约束**: ~50%消费者无法借贷(Campbell-Mankiw 1989),只能消费当期收入
- **近理性**: 优化成本小,但存在惯性;消费者"足够理性"但不完全

这两种解释对政策含义截然不同:若 liquidity constraint,政策应放松信贷;若 near-rationality,政策应简化决策环境。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 习惯形成的挑战

若效用函数包含习惯(habit formation):
$$U(C_t, C_{t-1})$$
则消费变化可预测(向习惯水平回归),破坏随机游走。实证支持习惯形成:
- Fuhrer (2000): 习惯参数显著,改善DSGE拟合
- 宏观数据: 消费惯性大,对冲击的响应缓慢

但习惯形成与微观证据(个体消费变化不可预测)不完全一致。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 异质性代理人的聚合问题

随机游走模型假设代表性代理人,但:
- **Kaplan-Violante (2014)**: " wealthy hand-to-mouth" — 有财富但 illiquid,行为像受约束
- **HANK模型**: 异质性使aggregate消费响应与representative agent模型截然不同
- **AGGREGATION FALLACY**: 个体随机游走 ≠ aggregate随机游走

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 信息摩擦与理性预期的现实性

随机游走假设消费者:
- 知道真实经济模型
- 能完美处理所有信息
- 无认知成本

现实中:
- **专业预测者**也犯错
- **消费者调查**显示预期高度分散
- **注意力限制**: 消费者关注有限信息集

行为宏观经济学(Angeletos-Lian 2018)发现,即使不完全理性的预期也能解释大部分宏观数据。

### 数据空白

1. **实时测试**: 政策制定时如何区分"可预测"vs"不可预测"的消费变化?
2. **微观-宏观桥**: 个体消费数据(CEX)与aggregate数据(NIPA)的不一致如何解释?
3. **中国的随机游走**: 高储蓄率、房地产主导的财富、社会保障不足使PIH+RE框架适用性存疑
4. **数字足迹**: 高频消费数据(信用卡、移动支付)能否更好测试随机游走?
