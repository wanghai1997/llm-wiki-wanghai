# 短期经济波动

**Short-run economic fluctuations**, commonly called **business cycles**, refer to the recurrent but irregular up-and-down movements in aggregate economic activity—measured primarily by real GDP, employment, and industrial production.

## Three Key Facts About Economic Fluctuations

Mankiw 9e (Ch 10-1) emphasizes three stylized facts that any theory of short-run fluctuations must explain:

1. **GDP fluctuations are irregular and unpredictable**: Recessions and expansions do not follow a fixed schedule. The National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER) identifies business cycle turning points retrospectively, not in real time.

2. **Unemployment is countercyclical**: When GDP falls, unemployment rises; when GDP recovers, unemployment falls—with a lag (Okun's law).

3. **Macroeconomic variables move together, but with different timing**:
   - **Procyclical variables**: Investment, consumption of durable goods, corporate profits (rise in booms, fall in recessions)
   - **Countercyclical variables**: Unemployment, inventory-to-sales ratios
   - **Leading indicators**: Stock prices, new building permits, average weekly manufacturing hours
   - **Lagging indicators**: Unemployment rate, duration of unemployment, commercial lending

## Classical vs. Keynesian Perspectives

| Dimension | Classical (Long Run) | Keynesian (Short Run) |
|---|---|---|
| Price flexibility | Fully flexible | Sticky / rigid |
| Output determination | Supply side (factors of production) | Demand side (aggregate spending) |
| Unemployment | Voluntary / frictional | Involuntary, due to deficient demand |
| Policy role | Limited (markets self-correct) | Active fiscal and monetary stabilization |
| Analytical framework | [[古典宏观长期模型]] | [[AD-AS模型]], [[IS-LM模型]] |

Mankiw 9e adopts the **neoclassical synthesis**: classical for the long run (Part II-III), Keynesian for the short run (Part IV).

## From Facts to Models

Ch 10 introduces the [[AD-AS模型]] as the organizing framework for understanding fluctuations:
- **Aggregate Demand (AD)**: The negative relationship between the price level and the quantity of output demanded
- **Aggregate Supply (AS)**: The positive (short-run) or vertical (long-run) relationship between the price level and the quantity of output supplied
- **Equilibrium**: The intersection of AD and AS determines the short-run level of output and prices

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 古典二分法 vs Keynesian 非二分法:教材的"双重标准"

Mankiw 9e 在同一本教材中使用两套互不兼容的均衡概念:
- Part II-III (Ch 3-9): 长期价格完全灵活,产出由供给面决定
- Part IV (Ch 10-13): 短期价格完全粘性,产出由需求面决定

**问题**:教材从未解释价格从粘性到灵活的**过渡机制**是什么。这是新凯恩斯主义文献的核心问题(Calvo定价、菜单成本、协调失灵),但教材将其简化为"短期看Keynes,长期看古典"的时间分割,回避了微基础。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 美国经济波动的"案例霸权"

Ch 10 的全部波动事实使用美国 GDP/失业率数据;Case Study 包括大萧条(美国)、2008金融危机(美国)、1990s繁荣(美国)。非美案例极少(日本流动性陷阱是主要例外)。欧盟、新兴市场、中国的短期波动模式(如中国1990s末国企改革下岗潮、2008后"四万亿"刺激)被边缘化。

### 数据与方法论的空白

1. **金融加速器**: Bernanke-Gertler-Gilchrist (1999) 发现信贷市场摩擦会放大经济波动——这是2008危机后的共识,但教材完全未出现
2. **现代周期测度**: 传统NBER周期判定是否仍适用于服务业占主导的21世纪经济?服务业就业波动远小于制造业
3. **全球金融危机后的新常态**: 2008后发达经济体经历"长期停滞"(secular stagnation),GDP增长持续低于潜在水平,传统AD-AS框架是否充分?
4. **新冠疫情冲击(2020)**: 教材2016年版无法涵盖 supply+demand 双重冲击同时发生的场景,以及财政货币化(MMT实践)的争议
