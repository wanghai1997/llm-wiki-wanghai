# 技术进步与索洛模型

The basic [[Solow增长模型]] without technological progress predicts that per-capita income converges to a steady state with **zero long-run growth**—a prediction contradicted by two centuries of sustained per-capita income growth in industrialized economies. The resolution is to introduce **labor-augmenting technological progress**, which allows the model to generate perpetual growth in living standards.

## Labor-Augmenting Technological Progress

### Definition

**Labor-augmenting** (or Harrod-neutral) technological progress raises the effective labor input:

$$Y = F(K, L \times E)$$

where $E$ is the efficiency of labor, growing at exogenous rate $g$:

$$\dot{E}/E = g$$

The term $L \times E$ is called **effective labor**.

### Why Labor-Augmenting?

The model requires labor-augmenting progress (rather than capital-augmenting or Hicks-neutral) to be consistent with a **balanced growth path** where:
- The capital-output ratio $K/Y$ is constant
- The return to capital $r$ is constant
- Factor shares are constant

These are the **Kaldor stylized facts** (see [[平衡增长路径]]). Only labor-augmenting progress is consistent with all three simultaneously in the neoclassical framework (Uzawa 1961).

### Alternative Forms

| Type | Production Function | Compatible with BGP? |
|---|---|---|
| Hicks-neutral | $Y = A(t)F(K,L)$ | Only if $Y$ is Cobb-Douglas |
| Capital-augmenting | $Y = F(B(t)K, L)$ | Only if $\dot{B}/B = 0$ in steady state |
| Labor-augmenting | $Y = F(K, E(t)L)$ | Yes (general production function) |

## Modified Capital Accumulation

Define variables **per effective worker**:
- $\tilde{k} = K/(L \cdot E)$ = capital per effective worker
- $\tilde{y} = Y/(L \cdot E) = f(\tilde{k})$ = output per effective worker

The capital accumulation equation becomes:

$$\Delta \tilde{k} = sf(\tilde{k}) - (\delta + n + g)\tilde{k}$$

The additional term $g\tilde{k}$ captures the need for "technology-adjusted" break-even investment: even if physical capital per worker were constant, the effective capital stock must grow to keep up with rising labor efficiency.

## Steady State with Technological Progress

The steady state $\tilde{k}^*$ satisfies:

$$sf(\tilde{k}^*) = (\delta + n + g)\tilde{k}^*$$

### Key Properties

At $\tilde{k}^*$:
- **Output per effective worker** is constant: $\tilde{y}^* = f(\tilde{k}^*)$
- **Output per actual worker** grows at rate $g$: $y = Y/L = E \cdot \tilde{y}^*$, so $\dot{y}/y = g$
- **Total output** grows at rate $n + g$: $\dot{Y}/Y = n + g$
- **The capital-output ratio** is constant: $K/Y = \tilde{k}^*/f(\tilde{k}^*)$

**Crucial insight**: The steady state now features **positive per-capita growth** at rate $g$, driven entirely by exogenous technological progress. Capital accumulation alone cannot sustain per-capita growth; only technology can.

## Policy Implications

Since the long-run growth rate $g$ is **exogenous** in the Solow framework:
- Policies that raise $s$ or lower $n$ raise the **level** of the balanced growth path but not its **slope**
- Only policies that raise $g$ raise long-run growth
- But the model provides no mechanism for how $g$ is determined—it is simply assumed

This unsatisfying feature motivated the development of **endogenous growth theory** (see [[内生增长理论]]), in which $g$ is determined by R&D investment, human capital accumulation, or other economic decisions.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION] 技术进步的"天降甘露"假设
Solow 模型将技术进步视为完全外生的参数 $g$，不解释其来源。这被批评为 "manna from heaven" (天降甘露)——技术好像凭空出现，与经济决策无关。这一假设在 1950s-60s 或许可接受（当时对创新的经济学理解有限），但在 Romer (1990) 后已成为严重缺陷。教材在 Ch 9-1 使用外生 $g$，然后在 Ch 9-4 转向内生增长，但二者之间的过渡并不平滑：学生可能困惑于为什么教材先教一个"错误"的模型，再教"修正"。

### [BIAS] 劳动增强型技术进步的形式选择
教材假设技术进步是劳动增强型（Harrod-neutral），因为这能保证平衡增长路径的存在。但这是否是技术进步的实际形式？
- 大量实证研究表明，技术进步具有**技能偏向性**（skill-biased），提高了高技能工人的相对生产率（Katz-Murphy 1992）
- **资本增强型**技术进步（计算机、自动化）可能同样重要，甚至更重要
- Uzawa 定理表明，只有劳动增强型进步能与任意生产函数下的 BGP 兼容，但这是一种数学便利，不一定反映现实

### 数据空白
- **技术进步的测度**：TFP 增长被广泛用作 $g$ 的代理，但 TFP 残差包含了测量误差、要素利用率变化、资源配置改善等多种成分，不纯粹是"技术进步"
- **2010s 生产率减速**：发达经济体在 2008 危机后经历了显著的 TFP 增长放缓（Robert Gordon, *The Rise and Fall of American Growth* 2016）。如果 $g$ 是外生常数，这种减速无法解释
- **AI 与通用目的技术**：AI 作为通用目的技术（GPT）是否已经进入生产函数？Brynjolfsson 等提出的"GDP-B"（GDP 未捕捉的免费数字服务价值）挑战了标准增长核算框架
