# 2008金融危机

The **financial crisis of 2008**—also known as the **Global Financial Crisis** or **Great Recession**—was the most severe economic contraction since the Great Depression. Triggered by the collapse of the US housing bubble, it propagated through the global financial system, causing a worldwide recession, massive government interventions, and a protracted recovery.

## Timeline of the Crisis

### Phase 1: The Housing Bubble (2002–2006)
- Fed funds rate cut to 1% (2003) after dot-com bust
- Subprime mortgage lending explodes
- Securitization: mortgages packaged into MBS, CDOs, CDO-squared
- Credit rating agencies assign AAA ratings to risky tranches
- House prices rise 60%+ nationally

### Phase 2: The Subprime Meltdown (2007)
- February: HSBC announces $10.5B subprime losses
- June: Bear Stearns hedge funds collapse
- August: BNP Paribas freezes three funds—interbank lending seizes

### Phase 3: Systemic Collapse (2008)
- March: Bear Stearns acquired by JPMorgan (Fed backstop)
- September 7: Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac placed in conservatorship
- September 15: **Lehman Brothers bankruptcy**—the tipping point
- September 16: AIG rescued ($85B Fed credit line)
- September 29: Dow Jones falls 777 points; global panic
- October: TARP enacted ($700B bank rescue fund)

### Phase 4: Global Recession (2008–2009)
- US GDP: -4.3% (Q4 2008), -4.6% (Q1 2009)
- Unemployment: rises from 5% to 10% (Oct 2009)
- Global trade collapses 15% in 2009
- Europe: sovereign debt crisis (Greece, Ireland, Spain)

### Phase 5: Policy Response (2008–2014)
- **Federal Reserve**: Funds rate cut to 0–0.25%; QE1 ($600B MBS purchases), QE2, QE3
- **Fiscal**: ARRA ($787B stimulus, 2009)
- **Regulation**: Dodd-Frank Act (2010)
- **International**: G20 coordinated stimulus; IMF emergency lending

## Transmission Mechanisms

### 1. Housing → Financial Institutions
- Mortgage defaults → MBS/CDO losses
- Banks face insolvency; interbank lending freezes
- Shadow banking system (repo, ABCP) collapses

### 2. Financial Institutions → Real Economy
- Credit freeze: firms cannot borrow for operations
- Consumer credit tightens
- Stock market falls 50%+ (Oct 2007–Mar 2009)
- Wealth destruction: $16 trillion household net worth lost

### 3. Global Spillovers
- European banks held US MBS
- Emerging market capital flows reverse
- Trade finance collapses
- Commodity prices crash

## The Role of Key Actors

| Actor | Role | Controversy |
|---|---|---|
| **Mortgage originators** | Made loans to unqualified borrowers | Predatory lending; "liar loans" |
| **Investment banks** | Packaged and sold toxic securities | Conflict of interest; misleading ratings |
| **Credit rating agencies** | Assigned AAA to risky products | Rating shopping; paid by issuers |
| **AIG** | Sold CDS "insurance" on MBS | Unhedged exposure; systemic risk |
| **Federal Reserve** | Kept rates low pre-crisis; lender of last resort | Contributed to bubble; moral hazard from bailouts |
| **Government** | Encouraged homeownership (Community Reinvestment Act) | Did regulation fail, or was deregulation the problem? |

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 教材的"技术性"叙事偏差

教材将危机呈现为"住房泡沫+金融传染"的技术性问题，但忽略了：
- **结构性根源**: 1980s以来金融去监管、收入分配恶化、全球失衡
- **政治经济学**: 金融行业的政治影响力推动去监管(Glass-Steagall repeal 1999)
- **分配维度**: 危机后果(失业、财富损失、不平等加剧)轻描淡写

### [CONTRADICTION-2] QE的效果争议

教材将QE呈现为成功政策，但：
- 对实体经济的传导微弱：银行持有超额准备金而非放贷
- 分配后果严重：资产价格通胀使富人受益
- 长期副作用未知：扭曲的资本配置、僵尸企业、金融脆弱性积累

### [CONTRADICTION-3] Dodd-Frank的有效性存疑

- "太大而不能倒"问题未根本解决(2023 SVB危机)
- 监管复杂性创造合规成本，可能阻碍中小银行竞争
- 影子银行体系(非银行金融机构)增长更快，监管更少

### 数据空白

1. **反事实**: 没有大规模干预会发生什么？第二次大萧条还是更快恢复？
2. **长期生产率影响**: 金融危机是否降低了长期增长潜力？
3. **行为因素的量化**: 恐慌、羊群效应在危机传播中的确切贡献难以测量
4. **regional-bias**: 教材几乎完全从美国视角叙述。欧洲主权债务危机、中国4万亿刺激、新兴市场资本外逃的深度不同。

### [regional-bias] 中国应对 2008 危机的经验

中国 2008-2010 应对金融危机的特征显著不同于美国 / 欧洲,深刻塑造了后续 [[孙国峰]] 2021 的 [[现代货币政策框架]]:

| 维度 | 美国 / 欧洲 | 中国 |
|---|---|---|
| 危机源 | 内生(住房 + 金融衍生品)| 外生(出口需求骤降)|
| 财政响应 | TARP 7000 亿 + ARRA 7870 亿 | "**4 万亿"经济刺激计划**(2008-11)|
| 货币响应 | QE1/2/3 + 零利率 | 大幅降息 + 信贷投放 + 准备金率下调 |
| 后果 | 缓慢复苏 + 资产价格泡沫 | 快速复苏 + 大量低效投资 + 地方政府债务 |
| 政策遗产 | Dodd-Frank + 宏观审慎工具 | [[宏观审慎评估MPA]](2016)+ 跨周期政策设计 |

**关键认识**:孙国峰 2021 指出"千招万招,管不住货币就是无用之招"——这是对 4 万亿后货币超发与产能过剩的反思,**主张更有纪律、更精准的货币政策**——是 [[结构性货币政策工具]] 的设计原点。

**理论意义**:中国 2008 经验催生了"双支柱"(货币 + 宏观审慎)思维 + [[跨周期政策设计]] 概念,部分内化了 Mankiw Ch 20 的金融加速器观点,但又强调结构性工具而非单纯总量工具。

详见 [[现代货币政策框架]] / [[跨周期政策设计]] / [[Milton Friedman]] 中"千招万招"语录在中国语境的引用。
