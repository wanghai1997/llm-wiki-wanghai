# 固定汇率下的政策效应

Under a **fixed exchange rate regime**, the central bank commits to maintaining the exchange rate at a predetermined level ($\bar{e}$). This commitment fundamentally alters the mechanics of macroeconomic policy compared to a floating rate system.

## How a Fixed Exchange Rate Works

To maintain the peg at $\bar{e}$:
- If market pressure pushes $e > \bar{e}$ (depreciation pressure), the central bank **sells foreign reserves** and **buys domestic currency**
- If market pressure pushes $e < \bar{e}$ (appreciation pressure), the central bank **buys foreign reserves** and **sells domestic currency**

These interventions automatically change the domestic money supply, making monetary policy endogenous to exchange rate defense.

## Fiscal Policy Under Fixed Rates

### Expansionary Fiscal Policy ($\Delta G > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Government spending increases → IS* shifts right
2. Upward pressure on domestic interest rate
3. Capital inflows attracted by higher returns
4. Pressure for currency appreciation ($e \downarrow$)
5. Central bank **sells domestic currency / buys foreign reserves** to defend the peg
6. Money supply increases ($M \uparrow$) → LM* shifts right
7. Output increases further ($Y \uparrow$)

**Result**: Fiscal policy is **highly effective** under fixed rates. The central bank passively accommodates fiscal expansion by expanding the money supply.

```
Exchange Rate (e)
    │
    │  ē (fixed)
    │══════════════════════════
    │         │
    │    LM₁* │ LM₂*
    │         │   │
    │    IS₁* │   │ IS₂*
    │       / │   │   /
    │      /  │   │  /
    └─────/───┼───┼─/──→ Output (Y)
           Y₁     Y₂
```

## Monetary Policy Under Fixed Rates

### Attempted Expansionary Monetary Policy ($\Delta M > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Central bank attempts to increase money supply → LM* shifts right
2. Downward pressure on domestic interest rate
3. Capital outflows seeking higher foreign returns
4. Pressure for currency depreciation ($e \uparrow$)
5. Central bank must **sell foreign reserves / buy domestic currency** to defend the peg
6. Money supply contracts back to original level ($M \downarrow$) → LM* shifts back left

**Result**: Monetary policy is **completely ineffective** under fixed rates. Any attempt to change the money supply is automatically undone by reserve flows.

**The fundamental constraint**: Under fixed rates, the central bank loses control of its money supply. Monetary policy must be subordinated to exchange rate defense.

## Devaluation and Revaluation

A country can change the peg level:
- **Devaluation**: Lowering $\bar{e}$ (currency becomes cheaper in foreign currency terms)
  - Expands net exports → IS* shifts right
  - Increases money supply (to prevent appreciation back to old peg) → LM* shifts right
  - Output increases
  - Equivalent to an expansionary monetary policy that "sticks"

- **Revaluation**: Raising $\bar{e}$ (currency becomes more expensive)
  - Contracts net exports → IS* shifts left
  - Decreases money supply → LM* shifts left
  - Output decreases

## Real-World Examples

### Hong Kong (1983–present)
- Currency board pegging HKD to USD at 7.80
- No independent monetary policy; interest rates track US rates
- Fiscal policy is the primary stabilization tool
- Survived the 1997 Asian financial crisis but experienced severe deflation 1998–2003

### Argentina (1991–2001)
- Currency board pegging peso to USD at 1:1
- Initially successful in ending hyperinflation
- Lost monetary independence; couldn't respond to 1998–2001 recession
- Forced devaluation and default in 2001–2002

### Eurozone
- Member countries have fixed rates against each other (irrevocably)
- ECB sets monetary policy for the entire zone
- Individual countries cannot use monetary policy for stabilization
- Fiscal policy is constrained by Stability and Growth Pact

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 货币政策的"完全无效"过于绝对

Mundell-Fleming预测固定汇率下货币政策完全无效,但现实中:
- ** sterilized intervention **:央行可以通过冲销操作(卖出国债抵消外汇储备变动)部分维持货币控制——但长期不可持续
- **资本管制**:中国通过资本账户管制部分维持货币政策独立性
- **暂时性**:短期中,如果资本流动不完全,货币政策在 peg 崩溃前可能有短暂效果

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 固定汇率的"稳定器"功能被高估

教材呈现固定汇率为可信的承诺机制,但:
- **投机攻击**:若市场怀疑 peg 的可持续性,可能引发自我实现的危机(Krugman 1979第一代危机模型;Obstfeld 1994第二代模型)
- **政策僵化**:无法应对不对称冲击(如欧元区南北差异)
- **逆周期资本流动**:繁荣时资本流入、衰退时流出,固定汇率下的货币政策被动 procyclical

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 财政政策的有效性受债务约束

固定汇率下财政政策虽"有效",但:
- 过度财政扩张导致债务累积
- 债务/GDP过高引发主权风险溢价
- 可能触发投机攻击(若市场怀疑财政可持续性)
- 阿根廷2001是典型案例:固定汇率+财政赤字→危机

教材未充分讨论固定汇率下财政扩张的债务可持续性约束。

### 数据空白

1. **中间汇率制度**:爬行钉住、篮子货币、区间浮动等"中间制度"的效应——教材仅讨论固定 vs 浮动二元对立
2. **汇率锚的选择**:钉住美元 vs 钉住一篮子货币 vs 钉住通胀目标——不同锚的宏观经济效应差异
3. **退出策略**:从固定汇率退出的经验(英国1992,阿根廷2001,瑞士2015)——教材未涉及
