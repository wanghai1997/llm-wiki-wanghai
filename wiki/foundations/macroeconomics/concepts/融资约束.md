# 融资约束

**Financial constraints** arise when the cost of external finance exceeds the cost of internal finance, causing firm investment to depend on cash flow and net worth rather than solely on profitable opportunities. This concept, developed by Fazzari, Hubbard, and Petersen (1988), explains why the simple neoclassical investment model fails empirically.

## The External Finance Premium

In a world of perfect information:
- Internal and external funds are perfect substitutes
- Investment depends only on $q$ or $MPK = r + \delta$

With asymmetric information:
- **Adverse selection**: Outside investors cannot distinguish good from bad projects
- **Moral hazard**: Borrowers may take excessive risks with other people's money
- **Agency costs**: Monitoring and contracting expenses raise the cost of external funds

$$\text{Cost of external finance} = r + \phi(\text{net worth}, \text{collateral})$$

where $\phi > 0$ and decreases with firm net worth.

## Cash Flow Sensitivity of Investment

Fazzari-Hubbard-Petersen (1988) tested whether investment depends on cash flow, controlling for $q$:

$$I = \alpha q + \beta \cdot \text{Cash Flow} + \epsilon$$

**Result**: $\beta > 0$ and significant, especially for:
- Small firms
- Young firms
- Firms with low dividend payout (likely constrained)
- Firms without bond market access

**Interpretation**: Constrained firms cannot borrow enough to fund all positive-NPV projects; their investment is limited by internal cash generation.

## The Financial Accelerator Mechanism

[[Ben Bernanke]]-Gertler-Gilchrist (1999) formalized how financing constraints amplify shocks:

1. Negative shock reduces asset prices and firm net worth
2. Lower net worth → higher external finance premium
3. Higher premium → reduced investment
4. Reduced investment → lower output → further asset price declines
5. **Feedback loop**: Initial shock is amplified

## Housing Investment and Collateral Constraints

For households:
- Home equity serves as collateral for borrowing
- Falling house prices reduce borrowing capacity
- This mechanism was central to the 2008 crisis transmission

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **Kaplan-Zingales (1997) 反论**: Even firms that appear unconstrained (high cash, high credit ratings) show cash flow sensitivity. They argue this reflects managerial discretion (empire building) rather than financing constraints. The FHP test may not cleanly identify constraints.
- **Gomes (2001) 校准批评**: A calibrated model with fixed adjustment costs can generate cash flow sensitivity without financing constraints—cash flow sensitivity is not unique evidence of constraints.
- **宏观 vs 微观不一致**: 微观层面融资约束的证据很强，但宏观层面（总投资的利率敏感性）仍主要由q/用户成本驱动。微观-宏观桥接仍不完整。
- **非正规金融**: 发展中国家的大量投资来自非正规渠道（家族、轮转储蓄、供应链信用）——标准"外部融资成本溢价"框架可能不适用。
- **加密货币与去中心化金融**: DeFi 借贷协议理论上可降低信息不对称和中介成本——但 2022 年 Terra/Luna 崩溃显示新形式的风险和约束。
