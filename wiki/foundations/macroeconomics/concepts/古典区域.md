# 古典区域

The **classical region** (or classical case) in the [[IS-LM模型]] corresponds to the extreme where the LM curve is **vertical**. In this region, money demand depends only on the level of income (transactions motive) and is completely insensitive to the interest rate. This represents the opposite extreme from the [[流动性陷阱]].

## LM Verticality: The Classical Case

In the LM equation:

$$\frac{M}{P} = L(r, Y)$$

The classical case occurs when $L_r = 0$—money demand does not respond to interest rate changes. This means:
- People hold money **only** for transactions
- The speculative motive for holding money (Keynes's liquidity preference) is absent
- Money demand is proportional to income: $L(Y) = kY$

**Result**: For any given $M/P$, there is only one level of $Y$ consistent with money market equilibrium, regardless of $r$. The LM curve is vertical.

```
Interest Rate (r)
    │
    │      LM (vertical)
    │        │
    │        │     IS
    │        │    /
    │        │   /
    │        │  /
    │        │ /
    └────────│/───────→ Output (Y)
             Ȳ
```

## Policy Implications

### Fiscal Policy: Completely Ineffective

- Expansionary fiscal policy (IS shifts right) raises interest rates but **does not change output**
- **Complete crowding out**: Higher $r$ reduces investment by exactly the amount that $G$ increased
- The composition of demand changes (more $G$, less $I$), but total $Y$ remains at the full-employment level
- Fiscal policy only affects the **allocation** of resources, not the **quantity**

### Monetary Policy: Highly Effective

- Expansionary monetary policy (LM shifts right) lowers interest rates and increases output
- Because money demand does not depend on $r$, the entire effect of $M/P$ works through $Y$
- The classical quantity theory holds: $MV = PY$, so changes in $M$ directly affect nominal spending

## Connection to Classical Theory

The classical case embodies the **classical dichotomy** and **monetary neutrality**:

**Classical dichotomy**: Real variables (output, employment) are determined by real factors (technology, capital, labor supply), independent of nominal variables (money supply, price level).

**Monetary neutrality**: Changes in the money supply affect only nominal variables (prices), not real variables (output).

In the classical region:
- Output is fixed at the full-employment level ($Y = \bar{Y}$)
- Fiscal policy cannot change $Y$ (consistent with classical supply-side determination)
- Monetary policy affects only prices (in the long run, when prices adjust)

## The Interest-Insensitive Money Demand

Why might money demand be interest-insensitive?
- **No financial innovation**: Limited alternative stores of value
- **Strict quantity theorists**: Money is only a medium of exchange
- **High inflation**: When inflation is high, the distinction between money and other nominal assets blurs

In practice, empirical estimates suggest money demand **is** sensitive to interest rates, making the classical case more of a theoretical limit than an empirical reality.

## The Spectrum of LM Slopes

| Region | LM Slope | Fiscal Policy | Monetary Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Liquidity trap** | Horizontal ($L_r \to -\infty$) | Fully effective | Ineffective |
| **Intermediate** | Upward-sloping | Partially effective | Partially effective |
| **Classical region** | Vertical ($L_r = 0$) | Completely ineffective | Highly effective |

The intermediate region represents the "normal" case analyzed in most of Mankiw's exposition.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 古典区域作为理论极端,缺乏实证支持

古典区域假设货币需求对利率完全不敏感,但:
- **实证估计**:几乎所有货币需求估计都发现显著的利率敏感性(Ball 2001, ECB 2003)
- **金融创新**:即使在"传统"经济体,货币市场基金、信用卡、电子支付降低了交易性货币需求
- **零利率时期**:当利率接近零时,货币需求对利率变化变得极为敏感——与古典假设相反

古典区域更像是一个理论基准而非现实描述,但教材有时将其与"长期"混为一谈,暗示长期中经济自动处于古典区域。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 古典区域与长期均衡的混淆

教材在讨论"长期中经济回到LRAS"时,有时暗示长期对应于古典区域。但:
- **长期**:价格完全灵活,产出由供给面决定——对应于AD-AS中LRAS垂直
- **古典区域**:货币需求对利率不敏感——是IS-LM中的一个假设,与"长期"无必然联系

即使在短期,如果央行以利率为目标(而非货币供应量),LM的古典垂直性也可能出现——但这并不意味着"长期"已经到达。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 财政政策在"正常时期"的挤出被夸大

IS-LM预测在接近古典区域时财政政策完全挤出,但实证:
- **美国1990s**:财政紧缩+货币宽松的成功暗示财政政策有显著效应
- **2008后**:巨额财政赤字伴随极低利率——没有挤出
- **日本**:政府债务/GDP>260%,利率仍接近零——没有挤出

这些现象暗示IS-LM在正常时期的挤出效应预测可能系统性过强。

### 数据空白

1. **货币需求函数的稳定性**:Goldfeld (1976) 发现1970s"失踪货币",此后货币需求函数一直不稳定
2. **利率敏感性的时变性**:零利率时期货币需求对利率变化更敏感,而非更不敏感
3. **央行操作框架的影响**:利率目标制下,LM的古典垂直性可能以不同形式出现
