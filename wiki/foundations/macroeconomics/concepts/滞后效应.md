# 滞后效应

**Hysteresis** (滞后效应) in macroeconomics refers to the phenomenon where temporary shocks have permanent effects on the natural rate of unemployment. Unlike the [[自然率假说]], which assumes the economy always returns to a fixed $u^n$ in the long run, hysteresis implies that prolonged periods of high unemployment can **permanently raise** the natural rate, leaving lasting "scars" on the economy.

## The Core Mechanism

### Blanchard-Summers (1986)

[[Olivier Blanchard]] and [[Lawrence Summers]] formalized the hysteresis hypothesis in their 1986 paper "Hysteresis and the European Unemployment Problem." They proposed three channels through which temporary high unemployment becomes permanent:

**1. Insider-Outsider Dynamics**
- Employed workers ("insiders") negotiate wages without considering the unemployed ("outsiders")
- Long-term unemployment erodes workers' skills and bargaining power
- Even when demand recovers, the unemployed are no longer competitive

**2. Capital-Labor Substitution**
- During prolonged recessions, firms invest in labor-saving capital
- When recovery comes, fewer workers are needed for the same output
- The labor demand curve shifts inward permanently

**3. Wage-Setting Institutions**
- High unemployment initially weakens unions
- But persistent unemployment may lead to defensive union behavior (protecting insiders)
- Labor market rigidities increase rather than decrease

### Formal Representation

Instead of a fixed $u^n$, hysteresis models specify:

$$u^n_t = u^n_{t-1} + \gamma(u_{t-1} - u^n_{t-1})$$

where $\gamma > 0$ measures the degree of hysteresis. If $\gamma = 0$, we recover the standard natural rate hypothesis. If $\gamma > 0$, deviations from $u^n$ permanently shift $u^n$ itself.

## European Unemployment: The Classic Case

Hysteresis gained prominence as an explanation for **European unemployment persistence**:

| Period | Average EU Unemployment | US Unemployment |
|---|---|---|
| 1960s | 2–3% | 4–5% |
| 1970s | 4–5% | 6–7% |
| 1980s | 8–10% | 7–8% |
| 1990s | 9–11% | 5–6% |
| 2000s | 7–9% | 5–6% |
| 2010s | 9–11% | 5–7% |

European unemployment rose sharply in the 1970s–80s and **never fully returned** to pre-1970s levels. The natural rate hypothesis cannot explain this persistence.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 美国vs欧洲的分化

If hysteresis is a general phenomenon, why did the US avoid it?
- **Labor market flexibility**: US wages adjust faster; job creation/destroy rates higher
- **Geographic mobility**: Americans move across states for jobs; Europeans less so
- **Skill depreciation**: May be less severe in the US due to different training systems
- **Policy response**: US fiscal/monetary policy more aggressive in recessions

This suggests hysteresis is **not universal**—it depends on labor market institutions.

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 2008后的美国经验

The 2008 crisis tested hysteresis in the US:
- Unemployment peaked at 10% (2009)
- By 2019, it fell to 3.5%—below pre-crisis estimates of $u^n$
- This suggests **no** hysteresis in the US

But some argue the scars appeared elsewhere:
- **Labor force participation** never fully recovered (dropped from 66% to ~63%)
- **Wage growth** remained sluggish even at low unemployment
- **Regional devastation**: Some Rust Belt areas never recovered

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 滞后vs结构性变化

区分"滞后效应"和"结构性变化"困难:
- 技术变革(自动化、全球化)可能永久抬高 $u^n$
- 但这并非"滞后"——而是外生冲击
- 欧洲高失业可能反映的是去工业化和全球化,而非1970s石油危机的滞后效应

教材有时将两者混为一谈。

### [CONTRADICTION-4] 政策含义的矛盾

如果滞后效应存在:
- **扩张主义**:应积极刺激需求,防止短期失业变成长期失业
- **供给侧改革**:应改革劳动力市场,降低 $u^n$ 本身

这两种策略不同甚至矛盾。滞后效应理论本身不能确定哪种政策更优。

### 数据空白

1. **$\gamma$ 的估计**:滞后参数的大小和稳定性缺乏一致估计
2. **跨部门异质性**:制造业失业 vs 服务业失业的滞后效应是否不同?
3. **中国的滞后效应**:二元劳动力市场(农民工返乡)使滞后机制复杂化
4. **COVID-19的疤痕**:疫情导致的失业是否会产生持久效应?
