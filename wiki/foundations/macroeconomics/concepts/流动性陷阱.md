# 流动性陷阱

A **liquidity trap** occurs when nominal interest rates fall to near zero and monetary policy becomes ineffective because the central bank cannot lower rates further. The concept originates with [[John Maynard Keynes]] (*The General Theory*, 1936) and has become highly relevant since the 2008 financial crisis and the COVID-19 pandemic.

## The Concept in IS-LM

In the [[IS-LM模型]], a liquidity trap corresponds to a **horizontal LM curve**:

```
Interest Rate (r)
    │
    │         LM (horizontal at r ≈ 0)
    │═══════════════════════════
    │         /
    │        / IS
    │       /
    │      /
    └────/──────────→ Output (Y)
```

**Why LM becomes horizontal**:
- When $r \approx 0$, money and bonds become perfect substitutes
- People are indifferent between holding cash (yielding 0%) and short-term bonds (yielding ≈0%)
- Additional money supply is simply hoarded rather than spent or invested
- Money demand becomes infinitely elastic with respect to $r$

## Policy Implications

### Monetary Policy: Ineffective

Conventional monetary expansion ($M \uparrow$) cannot lower $r$ below zero:
- LM shifts right but remains anchored at $r \approx 0$
- No stimulus to investment or output
- The economy is "pushing on a string"

### Fiscal Policy: Fully Effective

With a horizontal LM, fiscal expansion does **not** raise interest rates:
- IS shifts right with no crowding out
- The fiscal multiplier equals the simple Keynesian cross multiplier: $1/(1-MPC)$
- This was the theoretical rationale for massive fiscal stimulus in 2008–2009 and 2020

## Historical and Contemporary Examples

### Japan (1990s–2010s)

Japan experienced the most prolonged liquidity trap in modern history:
- Bubble burst in 1990 → asset price collapse → deflationary expectations
- Bank of Japan cut rates to zero by 1999
- Despite QE starting in 2001, deflation and stagnation persisted for two decades
- Fiscal stimulus (infrastructure spending) prevented deeper collapse but accumulated massive debt

### United States (2008–2015)

- Fed cut federal funds rate to 0–0.25% in December 2008
- Maintained near-zero rates for seven years
- Launched three rounds of quantitative easing (QE1, QE2, QE3)
- Recovery was slow; inflation remained below target

### Eurozone (2014–present)

- ECB cut deposit facility rate below zero (−0.5% by 2019)
- Purchased sovereign bonds via QE
- Inflation remained persistently below 2% target

### COVID-19 (2020–2021)

- Global coordinated rate cuts to zero
- Massive fiscal stimulus combined with QE
- Inflation eventually surged in 2021–2022 as supply shocks hit recovering demand

## Unconventional Monetary Policy at the Zero Lower Bound

When conventional monetary policy hits the zero lower bound (ZLB), central banks have turned to:

### Quantitative Easing (QE)
- Central bank purchases long-term assets (government bonds, MBS, corporate bonds)
- Goal: Lower long-term yields, compress term premia, stimulate asset prices
- Effectiveness debated: Works through portfolio rebalancing and signaling

### Forward Guidance
- Central bank commits to keeping rates low for an extended period
- Shapes private-sector expectations about future policy
- Can lower long-term rates even if short-term rate is stuck at zero

### Negative Interest Rate Policy (NIRP)
- Charging banks for holding reserves at the central bank
- Implemented by ECB, Bank of Japan, Swiss National Bank
- Effectiveness limited by bank profitability concerns and cash hoarding

### Yield Curve Control (YCC)
- Central bank targets specific long-term yields (e.g., BoJ targeting 10-year JGB at 0%)
- Commits to unlimited purchases to defend the target

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 流动性陷阱的教材处理:Keynes的预测 vs 2008后现实

Ch 12-3讨论流动性陷阱时称"Japan ... has experienced a liquidity trap"和"the United States ... in 2008"。但教材对流动性陷阱的政策建议仍停留在传统Keynesian框架(财政政策有效),未讨论2008后的新工具(QE、负利率、前瞻指引)及其在IS-LM中的位置。

更关键的是,教材称"in the liquidity trap, monetary policy is ineffective"——但QE通过资产组合再平衡和信号渠道可能在零下限仍有效(这超出IS-LM的二维框架)。Christiano-Eichenbaum-Rebelo (2011)甚至在DSGE模型中发现,在ZLB时**财政乘数可能大于1**——与IS-LM的预测一致,但机制更复杂。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 流动性陷阱 vs 通缩螺旋

教材将流动性陷阱作为"利率无法进一步降低"的技术问题,但现实中它与**通缩预期**紧密相连:
- 如果公众预期未来价格下降,实际利率(名义利率-通胀预期)可能在名义利率为零时仍为正
- 这进一步抑制投资和消费
- 日本案例显示,ZLB+通缩预期可以持续数十年

教材未充分讨论"预期陷阱"——即央行即使采取激进政策,若无法改变通缩预期,仍可能失效。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 负利率的理论挑战

传统流动性陷阱假设名义利率下限为0,但:
- 瑞士、丹麦、欧元区实施了负政策利率
- 日本央行尝试过负利率
- 理论下限可能是现金持有成本,而非严格为零

这使得"零利率下限"的概念需要修正为"有效下限"(effective lower bound, ELB),教材仍使用传统"zero lower bound"表述。

### 数据空白

1. **QE的效应测度**:如何区分QE的资产价格效应 vs 信号效应 vs 信贷渠道效应?识别策略仍在发展中
2. **前瞻性指引的可信度**:若央行无法承诺未来政策(时间不一致性),前瞻指引失效
3. **退出策略**:如何从ZLB正常化?美联储2015-2018的尝试和2019年的"转向"显示退出极其困难
4. **数字货币与ZLB**:CBDC可能消除现金下限,允许大幅负利率——但这是否可取?
