# 宏观审慎政策

**Macroprudential policy** is the regulation of the financial system with the goal of preventing systemic risk—the risk that the failure of one institution or market segment triggers a cascading collapse. It contrasts with **microprudential** regulation, which focuses on the safety of individual institutions.

## Microprudential vs. Macroprudential

| Dimension | Microprudential | Macroprudential |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective** | Individual institution safety | Systemic stability |
| **Risk view** | Risk as exogenous to the institution | Risk as endogenous to the system |
| **Externalities** | Ignored | Central focus |
| **Key tools** | Capital requirements, liquidity ratios | Countercyclical buffers, LTV limits, stress tests |
| **Fallacy of composition** | "If each bank is safe, the system is safe" | Individual safety ≠ system safety |

## Key Macroprudential Tools

### 1. Countercyclical Capital Buffers (Basel III)

Banks must hold extra capital during credit booms:
- **Boom**: Build buffer (0–2.5% of risk-weighted assets)
- **Bust**: Release buffer to absorb losses and maintain lending

Rationale: Private banks do not internalize the social cost of collective deleveraging during downturns.

### 2. Loan-to-Value (LTV) and Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limits

- Restrict mortgage lending during housing booms
- Prevents household balance sheet fragility
- Used extensively in Hong Kong, Singapore, South Korea

### 3. Stress Testing

- Simulate severe but plausible adverse scenarios
- Ensure banks have sufficient capital to survive
- US: Annual Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR)

### 4. Systemically Important Financial Institutions (SIFIs)

- Higher capital requirements for "too big to fail" institutions
- Living wills (resolution plans)
- Additional supervision

### 5. Macroprudential Leverage Ratios

- Basel III introduced a non-risk-based leverage ratio (3% minimum)
- Addresses the problem that risk-weighted assets can be gamed

## The Debate Over Effectiveness

### Supportive Evidence
- LTV limits in Asia reduced housing boom volatility
- Spanish dynamic provisioning (pre-2008) built buffers during the boom
- Countercyclical buffers released in 2020 cushioned the COVID shock

### Skeptical Arguments
- **Leakage**: Regulated banks shift activity to shadow banking (less regulated)
- **Cross-border arbitrage**: Tight regulation in one country pushes activity elsewhere
- **Political economy**: Building buffers during booms is politically unpopular ("why are you restricting credit when the economy is growing?")
- **Measurement**: Identifying bubbles and systemic risk in real time is extremely difficult

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **监管套利**: 宏观审慎政策使受监管银行成本上升，资金流向非银行金融中介(影子银行)。2010s美国影子银行规模快速增长，部分是对Basel III的反应。
- **国际协调缺失**: 金融危机是全球性的，但宏观审慎政策主要是国家层面的。跨境资本流动可以绕过单一国家的LTV限制。
- **定义"系统性风险"的困难**: 2008前，AIG不被视为SIFI；货币市场基金不被视为银行。事后识别风险源容易，事前几乎不可能。
- **与货币政策的交互**: 低利率(货币政策)鼓励冒险→宏观审慎应收紧。但两个政策工具由不同机构(央行vs监管机构)执行，协调困难。
- **regional-bias**: 宏观审慎框架以Basel III和欧美实践为中心。新兴市场(资本流动波动大、金融体系以银行为主)可能需要不同的工具组合(如资本管制、外汇干预)。
- **regional-bias - 中国 MPA + 双支柱框架**:中国 PBoC 2016 创设 [[宏观审慎评估MPA]] 体系,把资本充足率、广义信贷增速、流动性、定价行为、资产质量、跨境业务、信贷政策执行等**七维度**纳入综合评估。[[孙国峰]] 2021 提出**"货币政策 + 宏观审慎政策"双支柱调控框架**,把宏观审慎政策与货币政策**显式协同**——这与 BIS 主张的两者分离立场不同。中国实践更接近"宏观审慎是央行任务"模式,与英国 FPC、欧洲 ESRB 等"独立机构"模式形成对照。详见 [[宏观审慎评估MPA]] / [[现代货币政策框架]]。[regional-bias]
