# 凯恩斯交叉模型

The **Keynesian cross** (or expenditure-output model) is the simplest model of income determination in the short run. It illustrates how equilibrium output is determined by the equality of planned expenditure and actual output, and provides the foundation for understanding the government spending multiplier.

## Model Setup

### Planned Expenditure

Planned expenditure ($PE$) is the total amount of spending that households, firms, and the government intend to undertake:

$$PE = C(Y - T) + I + G$$

For simplicity, the basic model assumes:
- Investment ($I$) is exogenous (fixed)
- Government purchases ($G$) and taxes ($T$) are policy variables
- Consumption depends on disposable income: $C = C(Y - T)$ with $0 < MPC < 1$

### The Equilibrium Condition

Equilibrium occurs when actual output equals planned expenditure:

$$Y = PE$$

If $Y > PE$, firms accumulate unwanted inventories and cut production.
If $Y < PE$, firms deplete inventories and increase production.

```
Planned Expenditure (PE)
    │
    │      PE = C + I + G
    │        /
    │      /
    │    /
    │  /    45° line (Y = PE)
    │/____________
    └──────────────→ Output (Y)
        Y*
```

## The Multiplier

Suppose government purchases increase by $\Delta G$. The initial round increases income by $\Delta G$. But this income becomes disposable income, of which a fraction $MPC$ is spent, generating further rounds of spending.

The total change in output is:

$$\Delta Y = \Delta G + MPC \cdot \Delta G + MPC^2 \cdot \Delta G + ... = \frac{\Delta G}{1 - MPC}$$

**The government purchases multiplier**:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta G} = \frac{1}{1 - MPC}$$

For a typical MPC of 0.75, the multiplier is 4. For MPC = 0.8, the multiplier is 5.

### The Tax Multiplier

A tax cut increases disposable income and consumption, but only a fraction ($MPC$) of the tax cut is spent:

$$\frac{\Delta Y}{\Delta T} = -\frac{MPC}{1 - MPC}$$

The tax multiplier is smaller in absolute value than the spending multiplier because some of the tax cut is saved.

### The Balanced Budget Multiplier

If government spending and taxes increase by the same amount ($\Delta G = \Delta T$):

$$\Delta Y = \frac{1}{1 - MPC} \Delta G - \frac{MPC}{1 - MPC} \Delta T = \Delta G$$

So the balanced budget multiplier equals 1.

## The Paradox of Thrift

If households decide to save more (consume less) at every income level, the PE line shifts down. Paradoxically:
- **Individual virtue**: Saving more is prudent for each household
- **Collective vice**: In the aggregate, reduced consumption lowers income, and because $S = Y - C - T$, total saving may not increase at all

This illustrates the fundamental Keynesian insight: **the fallacy of composition**—what is true for individuals need not hold for the economy as a whole.

## Limitations of the Keynesian Cross

The Keynesian cross assumes that:
1. Prices are completely fixed
2. Interest rates are constant
3. There is no crowding out of investment

These assumptions are relaxed in the [[IS-LM模型]], where higher output raises money demand and interest rates, reducing investment and dampening the multiplier.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 乘数的实证争议

教材给出的乘数公式是 $1/(1-MPC)$,但实证估计远为复杂:
- **Ramey (2011)**:美国国防支出乘数约0.6-1.2,远低于简单公式预测
- **Auerbach-Gorodnichenko (2012)**:乘数在衰退期约1.5-2.0,但在扩张期接近0
- **Barro-Redlick (2011)**:考虑战时数据,乘数可能接近0.5

教材使用单一乘数数值,未呈现时变性和状态依赖性。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 开放经济中的乘数泄漏

简单乘数公式假设封闭经济。在开放经济中:
- 增加的收入部分用于进口($\Delta NX < 0$)
- 开放经济乘数变为 $1/(1 - MPC + MPM)$,其中MPM为边际进口倾向
- 小型开放经济中乘数可能接近1

教材在Ch 11封闭经济框架中讨论乘数,Ch 13 Mundell-Fleming中财政乘数为0(浮动汇率)或正(固定汇率)——这两个结论需要更明确的衔接。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] Ricardian等价命题

Barro (1974) 提出:如果消费者是理性的且考虑代际预算约束,减税(而不减少支出)不会改变消费——因为消费者预期未来增税。此时税收乘数为0,政府购买乘数为1(因政府支出直接计入GDP)。

教材简要提及此可能性但未深入讨论,使得 Keynesian cross 的规范结论存在理论上的重大挑战。

### 数据空白

1. **MPC的异质性**:不同收入群体的MPC差异巨大——低收入家庭MPC接近1,富裕家庭接近0。加总MPC掩盖了重要的分配效应
2. **前瞻性行为**:现代消费理论(生命周期、持久收入)预测消费者会平滑消费,使短期MPC低于静态模型假设
3. **信贷约束**:流动性约束家庭的行为更接近Keynesian cross预测,但无约束家庭的行为不同
