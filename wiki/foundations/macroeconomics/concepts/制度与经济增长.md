# 制度与经济增长

**Institutions**—the formal and informal rules that govern economic, political, and social interactions—are increasingly recognized as the deepest determinant of long-run economic growth. While the [[Solow增长模型]] emphasizes capital accumulation and exogenous technology, and the [[Mankiw-Romer-Weil模型]] adds human capital, a growing body of evidence suggests that **institutional quality** explains why some countries fail to accumulate capital or adopt technology despite having the resources to do so.

## What Are Institutions?

Following North (1990), institutions include:

**Formal institutions**:
- Property rights and contract enforcement
- Legal system quality and judicial independence
- Constitutional constraints on executive power
- Electoral rules and democratic accountability
- Regulatory frameworks

**Informal institutions**:
- Social norms and trust
- Corruption and patronage networks
- Religious and cultural attitudes toward commerce
- Ethnic and kinship-based obligation systems

## The Acemoglu-Johnson-Robinson (AJR) Framework

The most influential empirical study is [[Daron Acemoglu]], Simon Johnson, and James Robinson (2001), "The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development."

### The Argument

1. **European colonization created institutional variation**:
   - In regions with **high settler mortality** (tropical Africa, Central America): Europeans established **extractive institutions** (resource extraction, forced labor, minimal property rights) designed to exploit native populations and natural resources with minimal settlement.
   - In regions with **low settler mortality** (North America, Australasia, temperate zones): Europeans established **settler institutions** (private property, checks on government, rule of law) resembling European frameworks.

2. **Institutional persistence**: These initial institutional choices persisted for centuries after independence. Extractive colonies tended to remain extractive; settler colonies maintained inclusive institutions.

3. **Causal identification**: Using **settler mortality rates** as an instrumental variable for current institutions, AJR estimated that institutions explain the bulk of modern cross-country income differences.

### Empirical Results

- Countries with better institutions (property rights, constraints on executive) have much higher per-capita income
- The AJR instrument (settler mortality) predicts institutional quality, which in turn predicts income
- The estimated effect is large: moving from the 25th to 75th percentile of institutional quality is associated with a several-fold increase in per-capita income

## Other Evidence

### Natural Experiments

- **North vs South Korea**: Same geography, culture, history until 1945. Divergent institutions (communist vs capitalist) produced a 15–20x income gap within 50 years.
- **Nogales, Mexico/Arizona**: Same city, split by a border. Different institutions produce dramatically different living standards.
- **Hong Kong**: British colonial institutions (rule of law, free markets) transformed a barren rock into a wealthy financial center.

### Case Study: European Retail Regulation

Mankiw Ch 9-4 discusses European restrictions on large retailers (protecting small shops) as an example of institutions/policy slowing creative destruction. Restrictions on Walmart-style entry reduce competitive pressure and protect inefficient incumbents.

## [CONTRADICTION-3] AJR 的方法论争议

教材 Ch 9-3 将 AJR 2001 作为解释跨国收入差异的主导理论呈现，但不提及其方法论争议：

### Albouy (2012) 的数据修正
- David Albouy reconstructed the settler mortality data used by AJR
- Found significant measurement errors and inconsistencies
- When corrected, the first-stage relationship between mortality and institutions weakens
- The estimated causal effect of institutions on income becomes smaller and less precisely estimated

### Sachs (2003) 的地理假说
- Jeffrey Sachs argues geography (malaria, soil quality, landlockedness) directly affects health and productivity
- The AJR instrument (settler mortality) may capture geographic factors as well as institutional ones
- If malaria makes Europeans sick and also reduces native productivity directly, the IV estimates conflate geography and institutions

### 制度的内生性
- Good institutions may be easier to sustain in rich countries (endogeneity)
- The causal arrow may run: income → institutions, not just institutions → income
- Colonial history is not randomly assigned; European powers may have chosen to settle where existing conditions were favorable

### 亚洲发展例外
- South Korea and Taiwan developed rapidly under authoritarian regimes with imperfect institutions
- China's growth under communist party rule challenges simple "good institutions → growth" causal chains
- Singapore's Lee Kuan Yew model combined market economics with authoritarian political control

教材将 AJR 呈现为"remarkable correlation"的解答，忽略 ongoing debate，构成显著的 [CONTRADICTION]。

## Policy Implications

If institutions are the deepest growth determinant, development policy should prioritize:
- **Property rights reform** (land titling, intellectual property)
- **Judicial reform** (speeding contract enforcement, reducing corruption)
- **Democratic accountability** (constraints on executive expropriation)
- **Decentralization** (matching governance scale to local conditions)

However:
- Institutional change is **slow and politically difficult**
- External imposition of institutions (colonialism, IMF conditionality) often fails
- **Path dependence** makes bad institutions sticky—elites benefit from extractive systems and resist reform

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 制度决定论的因果识别问题
教材选择性呈现 AJR 为 settled science，忽略 Albouy (2012) 的数据修正、Sachs (2003) 的地理竞争假说、以及亚洲发展例外。这使学生误以为制度决定论是 consensus，而实际上这是活跃 research frontier 上的 contested claim。

### [regional-bias] 殖民制度视角的欧美中心主义
AJR 框架将现代发展差异追溯至欧洲殖民者的制度选择，隐含了一种欧美中心主义：
- 非欧洲社会的 pre-colonial 制度（中国的科举制度、奥斯曼的米利特制度、非洲的部落治理）被简化为"缺失"或"不足"
- 成功的 post-colonial 制度创新（新加坡的廉政公署、卢旺达的性别配额、博茨瓦纳的部落议会转型）未获充分重视
- 殖民遗产是复杂的：印度继承了英国法治框架但也继承了行政僵化；非洲许多国家继承了 arbitrary borders 和 extractive 官僚体系

### 数据空白
- **制度的微观基础**：宏观层面的"制度质量指数"（WGI、ICRG）掩盖了巨大 heterogeneity。同一国家内，不同地区、不同行业的制度执行可能差异巨大
- **制度变迁的动态**：如何从 extractive 转向 inclusive institutions？Acemoglu-Robinson (*Why Nations Fail* 2012) 强调 critical junctures 和 political coalitions，但可操作的政策建议有限
- **中国之谜**：中国的制度（产权保护薄弱、法治不健全、腐败普遍）在标准指标上得分低，但实现了 40 年高速增长。这是否意味着"制度"的测度遗漏了某些维度（如地方政府竞争、政策实验灵活性）？
