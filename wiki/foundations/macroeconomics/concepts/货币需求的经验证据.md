# 货币需求的经验证据

Empirical estimation of money demand is central to monetary policy. If the demand for money is stable and predictable, central banks can control the money supply to achieve price stability. The history of empirical money demand research is a story of initial stability, followed by breakdown and ongoing uncertainty.

## The Goldfeld Era: Stability (1960s–1970s)

Stephen Goldfeld (1973) estimated a standard money demand function:

$$\ln(M/P) = \alpha + \beta \ln Y + \gamma i + \epsilon$$

| Parameter | Goldfeld (1973) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Income elasticity ($\beta$) | 0.5–0.7 | Less than unitary (Baumol-Tobin predicts 0.5) |
| Interest elasticity ($\gamma$) | -0.05 to -0.10 | Small but significant |

The model fit well for M1 in the US through the early 1970s.

## The Missing Money: Instability (1974–1980s)

Goldfeld (1976) documented a sharp divergence:
- Predicted M1 demand exceeded actual holdings by 5–10%
- The "case of the missing money" undermined confidence in monetary targeting

**Causes of instability**:
- **Financial innovation**: NOW accounts, ATS accounts, money market mutual funds blurred the M1 boundary
- **Interest rate deregulation**: Removal of Regulation Q allowed banks to pay interest on checking
- **Electronic payments**: Reduced the transactions demand for M1

## Post-2008: The Great Dislocation

The 2008 financial crisis introduced further structural breaks:
- **Massive excess reserves**: Fed QE expanded the monetary base 5×, but M2 grew modestly
- **Interest on excess reserves (IOER)**: Banks held reserves voluntarily; the money multiplier broke down
- **Velocity collapse**: $V = PY/M$ fell to historic lows

| Period | M2 Velocity | Characteristic |
|---|---|---|
| 1960–1980 | ~1.7 | Relatively stable |
| 1980–2008 | Declining | Financial innovation |
| 2008–2020 | ~1.1 | Collapse post-crisis |
| 2020–2023 | ~1.1–1.3 | Pandemic + fiscal transfer volatility |

## Implications for Monetary Policy

1. **Money supply targeting abandoned**: Central banks shifted from monetary aggregates to interest rate targeting
2. **Inflation targeting**: Focus on the price level rather than money growth
3. **Divisia indices**: Weighted monetary aggregates that account for different assets' "moneyness"

## 反面论点与数据空白

- **Lucas critique**: 任何估计的货币需求关系在政策制度变化时都会失效。Goldfeld的"稳定"只是特定制度下的结果。
- **CBDC的未知效应**: 央行数字货币可能从根本上改变货币需求的结构——如果CBDC与银行存款竞争，货币乘数和需求函数可能再次剧变。
- **加密货币的分流测度**: 稳定币(USDT流通量>$80B)是否应计入"货币需求"？现有M2统计不包含链上稳定币，但它们在跨境支付和新兴市场中的交易媒介功能日益重要。
- **regional-bias**: 货币需求研究以美国为主。欧元区(单一货币、多国财政政策)、中国(资本管制、数字人民币)、日本(现金文化持久)的经验与美国差异显著。
