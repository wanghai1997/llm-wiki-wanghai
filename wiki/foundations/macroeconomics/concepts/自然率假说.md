# 自然率假说

The **natural rate hypothesis**, developed independently by [[Milton Friedman]] (1968 AEA presidential address) and [[Edmund Phelps]] (1967–68), states that there is a unique "natural" rate of unemployment ($u^n$) determined by structural features of the labor market. In the long run, the economy returns to $u^n$ regardless of the inflation rate. Attempts to hold unemployment below $u^n$ through expansionary policy only produce accelerating inflation.

## The Friedman-Phelps Critique

### Historical Context

By the mid-1960s, the [[Phillips曲线]] had become the cornerstone of Keynesian demand management. Policymakers believed they faced a **stable, permanent tradeoff**: choose low inflation with high unemployment, or low unemployment with high inflation.

Friedman (1968) and Phelps (1967–68) demolished this view.

### The Core Argument

**Friedman's formulation** (1968 AEA address "The Role of Monetary Policy"):

> "There is always a temporary tradeoff between inflation and unemployment; there is no permanent tradeoff. The temporary tradeoff comes not from inflation per se, but from **unanticipated inflation**..."

The mechanism:
1. Expansionary monetary policy raises aggregate demand
2. Prices rise faster than expected ($\pi > \pi^e$)
3. Real wages fall; firms hire more workers; unemployment drops below $u^n$
4. But workers eventually recognize the inflation and demand higher nominal wages
5. Real wages return to equilibrium; employment falls back to $u^n$
6. **Result**: higher inflation, same unemployment

**Phelps's formulation** (1967 "Phillips Curves, Expectations of Inflation and Optimal Unemployment Over Time"):
- Developed a formal search-theoretic model of the labor market
- Firms and workers form expectations about inflation
- Only when $\pi \neq \pi^e$ does unemployment deviate from $u^n$
- Introduced the concept of the **expectations-augmented Phillips curve**

### The Expectations-Augmented Phillips Curve

$$\pi = \pi^e - \beta(u - u^n)$$

- If $\pi^e$ is fixed (short run): inverse relationship between $\pi$ and $u$
- If $\pi^e$ adjusts (long run): $u = u^n$, Phillips curve is **vertical**

**The accelerationist hypothesis**: To maintain $u < u^n$, policymakers must continuously **accelerate** inflation ($\pi$ must keep rising). Any fixed inflation rate is compatible only with $u = u^n$.

## What Determines the Natural Rate?

Friedman and Phelps argued $u^n$ reflects **real, structural** factors:
- Labor market institutions (unions, minimum wages, unemployment benefits)
- Demographics (age composition of workforce)
- Sectoral shifts (rate of structural change)
- Search and matching frictions
- Labor mobility and geographic flexibility

Crucially: $u^n$ is **independent of monetary policy** in the long run. Money is neutral.

## Empirical Validation: The 1970s

The natural rate hypothesis gained dramatic empirical support during the 1970s:

| Period | US Unemployment | Inflation | Observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1961–1969 | 4–5% | 1–6% | Stable short-run tradeoff |
| 1970–1973 | 5–6% | 3–8% | Stagflation begins |
| 1974–1975 | 8–9% | 9–12% | Oil shock + high inflation |
| 1979–1982 | 7–10.8% | 11–14% | Volcker disinflation |

The coexistence of high inflation and high unemployment (**stagflation**) was impossible under the original Phillips curve but predicted by Friedman-Phelps.

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 自然率本身是否稳定?

自然率假说假设 $u^n$ 由结构性因素决定、缓慢变化。但:
- **美国 $u^n$ 估计**:1960s约5-6%,1980s上升至6-7%,2010s回落至4-5%——大幅波动
- **欧洲 $u^n$ 上升**:1970s约3%,1990s约8-10%——结构性失业的持久增长
- **滞后效应** (hysteresis):见[[滞后效应]]——短期高失业可能永久提高 $u^n$

如果 $u^n$ 本身受需求管理政策影响,自然率假说的政策含义被削弱。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 劳动力市场制度的复杂性

教材将 $u^n$ 简化为"结构性",但不同制度下 $u^n$ 的决定机制截然不同:
- **北欧模式**:高工会密度+集中谈判→工资灵活、失业低(丹麦"弹性安全")
- **欧洲大陆模式**:中等工会密度+行业谈判→工资僵化、失业高
- **盎格鲁-撒克逊模式**:低工会密度→工资灵活但收入不平等高

简单的 $u^n$ 概念掩盖了制度差异。

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 自然率是否可观测?

$u^n$ 无法直接观测,只能估计。但估计方法充满争议:
- **HP滤波**:选择平滑参数主观性强
- **NAIRU估计** (非加速通胀失业率):Blanchard-Quah方法、Laubach-Williams方法结果差异大
- **实时 vs 事后估计**:政策制定时使用的 $u^n$ 估计往往事后被证明严重错误

2008年后,对 $u^n$ 的估计失误导致美联储过早加息(2015-2018)。

### 数据空白

1. **$u^n$ 的跨国比较**:缺乏系统性的 $u^n$ 估计方法论比较
2. **自然率的实时识别**:如何在政策制定时点准确识别 $u^n$,而非事后?
3. **中国的自然率**:二元劳动力市场(城镇vs农村)、户籍制度使 $u^n$ 概念难以直接应用
4. **AI与自动化对 $u^n$ 的影响**:技术变革是否正在结构性抬高 $u^n$?
