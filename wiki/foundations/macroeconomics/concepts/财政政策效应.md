# 财政政策效应

**Fiscal policy** refers to the government's use of spending ($G$) and taxation ($T$) to influence aggregate demand, output, and employment. In the [[IS-LM模型]], fiscal policy shifts the IS curve, affecting both output and interest rates.

## Expansionary Fiscal Policy

### Increase in Government Purchases ($\Delta G > 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Higher $G$ directly increases aggregate demand
2. IS curve shifts right by $\Delta G \times \frac{1}{1-MPC}$
3. New equilibrium: higher $Y$, higher $r$

**Effects**:
- Output increases: $\Delta Y > 0$
- Interest rate rises: $\Delta r > 0$
- **Crowding out**: Higher $r$ reduces private investment

**Crowding out magnitude**:
- Small if LM is flat (liquidity trap) or investment is insensitive to $r$
- Large if LM is steep or investment is highly sensitive to $r$

### Decrease in Taxes ($\Delta T < 0$)

**Mechanism**:
1. Higher disposable income ($Y - T$) increases consumption
2. IS curve shifts right by $\Delta T \times \frac{MPC}{1-MPC}$
3. New equilibrium: higher $Y$, higher $r$

**Effects**:
- Output increases, but by less than an equivalent spending increase
- Crowding out still occurs

## Contractionary Fiscal Policy

### Decrease in Government Purchases or Increase in Taxes
- IS shifts left
- Output falls, interest rate falls
- **Crowding in**: Lower $r$ stimulates private investment

## Fiscal Policy in Different Economic Conditions

### Normal Times
- Fiscal expansion stimulates output but raises interest rates
- Effectiveness depends on monetary policy response
- If the central bank accommodates (keeps $r$ constant), crowding out is avoided

### Liquidity Trap ($r \approx 0$)
- LM is horizontal
- Fiscal expansion is **fully effective** (no crowding out)
- Interest rates cannot rise because they are already at the zero lower bound
- This was the theoretical rationale for large fiscal stimulus in 2008–2009 and 2020

### Classical Case (Vertical LM)
- Fiscal expansion is **completely ineffective**
- Any IS shift is fully offset by interest rate increases
- Output remains at the full-employment level
- Only the composition of demand changes (more $G$, less $I$)

## Automatic Stabilizers

Fiscal policy effects occur partly through automatic mechanisms:
- **Progressive income tax**: Tax revenues fall automatically in recessions, cushioning disposable income
- **Unemployment insurance**: Benefits rise automatically when unemployment increases
- **Welfare programs**: Expand eligibility during downturns

Automatic stabilizers provide immediate countercyclical support without legislative delay.

## The US Experience: 1990s Boom

Mankiw highlights the 1990s as an example of policy interaction:
- **Clinton fiscal consolidation** (1993): Tax increases and spending restraint shifted IS left
- **Fed monetary easing**: Greenspan kept interest rates low, shifting LM right
- **Result**: Strong output growth with falling interest rates—an example of successful policy coordination

## 反面论点与数据空白

### [CONTRADICTION-1] 挤出效应的教材矛盾

Ch 11 Keynesian cross 预测政府购买乘数为 $1/(1-MPC)$ (约2-5),但 Ch 12 IS-LM 引入挤出效应后乘数大幅缩小。教材在 Ch 12-1 说"the increase in income is smaller than ... the Keynesian cross",但未给出定量估计。

更关键的是,Ch 12-1 的 1990s 案例(财政紧缩+货币扩张)隐含承认财政政策在正常情况下有显著挤出,这与 Ch 10 稳定化政策"政府应主动干预"的规范立场形成张力。

### [CONTRADICTION-2] 财政政策在现代宏观中的"复兴"与争议

2008 危机后,财政政策重新成为核心工具:
- **美国 ARRA (2009)**:约8000亿美元刺激
- **CARES Act (2020)**:约2万亿美元
- **Auerbach-Gorodnichenko (2012)**:乘数在衰退期可达1.5-2.0

但教材仍沿用传统框架,未充分呈现:
- 财政乘数的**状态依赖性**(衰退期 vs 扩张期)
- **现代货币理论(MMT)**的挑战(Wray, Kelton)
- **财政主导(fiscal dominance)**风险(高债务下的政策约束)

### [CONTRADICTION-3] 供给侧效应被系统忽略

教材将财政政策视为纯需求管理工具,但:
- **公共投资**(基础设施、教育、研发)可能提高长期生产率
- **税收结构**影响劳动供给、储蓄和创新
- **政府规模**本身可能通过效率/挤出渠道影响增长

这些供给侧效应使"扩张性财政政策"的净效应远超简单的AD移动。

### 数据空白

1. **财政乘数的时变性**:衰退期 vs 扩张期差异巨大,但教材使用单一数值
2. **分配效应**:财政刺激对不同收入群体的效应不同,加总分析掩盖重要差异
3. **国际溢出**:一国财政扩张通过贸易渠道影响他国,但教材以封闭经济为主
4. **中国经验**:中国"四万亿"刺激(2008)的效果和后遗症(地方政府债务、房地产泡沫)是独特案例,教材未涉及
